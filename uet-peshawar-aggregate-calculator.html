Frequently asked questionsAksar poochhe jane wale sawal

What is the UET Peshawar aggregate formula?UET Peshawar ka aggregate formula kya hai?

The formula is not written the same way in online sources. One place says FSc 70% and test 30%, another says FSc 30% and test 70%. That is why this calculator has both options and an option to write your own. Check the correct formula for this year from the official UET Peshawar prospectus or notice.Online sources me formula ek jaisa nahi likha. Ek jagah FSc 70% aur test 30% likha hai, dusri jagah FSc 30% aur test 70%. Is liye is calculator me dono option aur khud likhne ka option hai. Sahi aur is saal ka formula UET Peshawar ke official prospectus ya notice se check karein.

Are Matric marks counted at UET Peshawar?UET Peshawar me Matric ke marks lagte hain?

Some sources do not mention Matric. In this calculator the Matric weightage is set to 0; if your prospectus has a Matric weightage, change it.Kuch sources me Matric ka zikr nahi hai. Is calculator me Matric ka weightage 0 rakha gaya hai; agar aap ki prospectus me Matric ka weightage hai to usay badal lein.

How many total marks does the entry test have?Entry test ke total marks kitne hote hain?

Different sources say different things (such as 200 or 400). Enter the total marks of this year's paper.Alag sources me alag likha hai (jaise 200 ya 400). Jo is saal ke paper ke total marks hon wahi likhein.

Is this the final merit?Kya ye final merit hai?

No, this is only an estimate. For the actual merit list see the official UET Peshawar notice.Nahi, ye sirf andaza hai. Asal merit list ke liye UET Peshawar ka official notice dekhein.
