1. Newton's first law is also known as:

  • A) Law of acceleration
  • B) Law of inertia
  • C) Law of action and reaction
  • D) Law of gravitation
Show answer

Correct answer: B) Law of inertia
The first law says an object keeps its state (rest or motion) unless a net force acts on it. This is called the law of inertia.

1. Newton ka pehla law kis naam se bhi jana jata hai?

  • A) Law of acceleration
  • B) Law of inertia
  • C) Law of action and reaction
  • D) Law of gravitation
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: B) Law of inertia
Pehla law batata hai ke koi cheez apni halat (rest ya motion) tab tak nahi badalti jab tak us par net force na lage, isay law of inertia kehte hain.

2. What is the SI unit of force?

  • A) Newton
  • B) Joule
  • C) Watt
  • D) Pascal
Show answer

Correct answer: A) Newton
The SI unit of force is the newton (N). 1 N = 1 kg m/s².

2. Force ki SI unit kya hai?

  • A) Newton
  • B) Joule
  • C) Watt
  • D) Pascal
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: A) Newton
Force ki SI unit newton (N) hai. 1 N = 1 kg m/s².

3. A 5 kg object has an acceleration of 2 m/s². What force acts on it?

  • A) 2.5 N
  • B) 7 N
  • C) 3 N
  • D) 10 N
Show answer

Correct answer: D) 10 N
By the second law, F = ma = 5 × 2 = 10 N.

3. Ek 5 kg ke jism ka acceleration 2 m/s² hai. Us par kitni force lag rahi hai?

  • A) 2.5 N
  • B) 7 N
  • C) 3 N
  • D) 10 N
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: D) 10 N
Doosre law ke mutabiq F = ma = 5 × 2 = 10 N.

4. According to Newton's third law, action and reaction forces:

  • A) are equal and act on the same body
  • B) cancel each other because they act on the same body
  • C) are equal and opposite and act on different bodies
  • D) are always unequal
Show answer

Correct answer: C) are equal and opposite and act on different bodies
Action and reaction are equal and opposite, but they act on two different bodies, so they do not cancel each other.

4. Newton ke teesre law ke mutabiq action aur reaction forces:

  • A) barabar hoti hain aur ek hi jism par lagti hain
  • B) ek doosre ko khatam kar deti hain kyunke wo ek hi jism par hoti hain
  • C) barabar aur ulti simt mein hoti hain aur alag alag jismon par lagti hain
  • D) hamesha ek doosre se kam ya zyada hoti hain
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: C) barabar aur ulti simt mein hoti hain aur alag alag jismon par lagti hain
Action aur reaction ki force barabar aur ulti simt me hoti hain, lekin do alag jismon par lagti hain, isliye wo ek doosre ko cancel nahi karti.

5. The inertia of an object depends on its:

  • A) Speed
  • B) Mass
  • C) Colour
  • D) Position
Show answer

Correct answer: B) Mass
The greater the mass, the greater the inertia.

5. Kisi jism ki inertia kis par depend karti hai?

  • A) Us ki raftaar par
  • B) Us ke mass par
  • C) Us ke rang par
  • D) Us ki jagah par
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: B) Us ke mass par
Mass jitna zyada ho, inertia utni zyada hoti hai.

6. What is the formula for momentum?

  • A) p = mv
  • B) p = ma
  • C) p = m/v
  • D) p = F/t
Show answer

Correct answer: A) p = mv
Momentum is the product of mass and velocity: p = mv. Unit: kg m/s.

6. Momentum ka formula kya hai?

  • A) p = mv
  • B) p = ma
  • C) p = m/v
  • D) p = F/t
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: A) p = mv
Momentum mass aur velocity ka hasil e zarb hai, yani p = mv. Unit kg m/s.

7. Which law explains why a rocket rises?

  • A) Newton's first law
  • B) Newton's second law
  • C) Boyle's law
  • D) Newton's third law
Show answer

Correct answer: D) Newton's third law
The rocket pushes gas downward (action) and the gas pushes the rocket upward (reaction). This is the third law.

7. Raaket ke oopar jane ki wajah kaun sa law hai?

  • A) Newton ka pehla law
  • B) Newton ka doosra law
  • C) Boyle ka law
  • D) Newton ka teesra law
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: D) Newton ka teesra law
Raaket gas ko neeche dhakelta hai (action), aur gas raaket ko oopar dhakelti hai (reaction). Ye teesra law hai.

8. If the net force on an object is zero, the object:

  • A) always stops
  • B) keeps speeding up
  • C) stays at rest or keeps moving in a straight line at constant speed
  • D) starts to rotate
Show answer

Correct answer: C) stays at rest or keeps moving in a straight line at constant speed
By the first law, velocity does not change when the net force is zero.

8. Agar kisi jism par lagne wali net force zero ho, to jism:

  • A) hamesha rukk jata hai
  • B) tezi se barhta jata hai
  • C) rest ya yaksan raftaar se seedhi line me chalta rehta hai
  • D) ghoomne lagta hai
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: C) rest ya yaksan raftaar se seedhi line me chalta rehta hai
Pehle law ke mutabiq net force zero ho to velocity nahi badalti.

9. When a bus brakes suddenly, passengers lurch forward. Why?

  • A) Gravity
  • B) Inertia
  • C) Friction
  • D) Buoyancy
Show answer

Correct answer: B) Inertia
The passengers' bodies tend to stay in their state of motion, so they move forward.

9. Bus achanak brake lagaye to musafir aage ki taraf jhuk jate hain. Iski wajah kya hai?

  • A) Gravity
  • B) Inertia
  • C) Friction
  • D) Buoyancy
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: B) Inertia
Musafiron ka jism apni motion ki halat me rehna chahta hai, isliye aage jhukta hai.

10. What force is needed to give a 2 kg object an acceleration of 3 m/s²?

  • A) 6 N
  • B) 1.5 N
  • C) 5 N
  • D) 0.67 N
Show answer

Correct answer: A) 6 N
F = ma = 2 × 3 = 6 N.

10. 2 kg ke jism ko 3 m/s² ka acceleration dene ke liye kitni force chahiye?

  • A) 6 N
  • B) 1.5 N
  • C) 5 N
  • D) 0.67 N
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: A) 6 N
F = ma = 2 × 3 = 6 N.

11. What is the weight of a 10 kg object on Earth? (g = 9.8 m/s²)

  • A) 10 N
  • B) 9.8 N
  • C) 980 N
  • D) 98 N
Show answer

Correct answer: D) 98 N
Weight W = mg = 10 × 9.8 = 98 N.

11. 10 kg ke jism ka weight zameen par kitna hoga? (g = 9.8 m/s²)

  • A) 10 N
  • B) 9.8 N
  • C) 980 N
  • D) 98 N
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: D) 98 N
Weight W = mg = 10 × 9.8 = 98 N.

12. One newton is equal to:

  • A) 1 kg m/s
  • B) 1 g m/s²
  • C) 1 kg m/s²
  • D) 1 kg m²/s²
Show answer

Correct answer: C) 1 kg m/s²
From F = ma, 1 N = 1 kg × 1 m/s² = 1 kg m/s².

12. 1 newton barabar hai:

  • A) 1 kg m/s
  • B) 1 g m/s²
  • C) 1 kg m/s²
  • D) 1 kg m²/s²
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: C) 1 kg m/s²
F = ma se 1 N = 1 kg × 1 m/s² = 1 kg m/s².
