Question 1. Solve x² − 7x + 10 = 0.Sawal 1. x² − 7x + 10 = 0 hal karein.

Show sample answerNamoona jawab dekhein

Factors: (x − 2)(x − 5) = 0, so x = 2 or x = 5.Factors: (x − 2)(x − 5) = 0, is liye x = 2 ya x = 5.

Question 2. Find the area of a triangle with base 10 and height 6.Sawal 2. Base 10 aur height 6 wale triangle ka area nikalein.

Show sample answerNamoona jawab dekhein

Area = ½ × base × height = ½ × 10 × 6 = 30 square units.

Question 3. Write the Pythagoras theorem.Sawal 3. Pythagoras theorem likhein.

Show sample answerNamoona jawab dekhein

In a right-angled triangle, the square of the hypotenuse equals the sum of the squares of the other two sides: c² = a² + b².Right angle triangle mein hypotenuse ka square baqi do pehlon ke squares ke majmue ke barabar hota hai: c² = a² + b².
