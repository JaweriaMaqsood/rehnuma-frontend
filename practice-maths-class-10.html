Hissa 1: MCQs (10)

Q1. x² − 5x + 6 = 0 ke roots kya hain?

  1. 1 aur 6
  2. 2 aur 3
  3. −2 aur −3
  4. 5 aur 1
Jawab dekhein

B) 2 aur 3

Q2. x² + 2x + 1 ka discriminant kitna hai?

  1. 4
  2. 1
  3. 0
  4. −4
Jawab dekhein

C) 0

Q3. (a + b)² barabar hai:

  1. a² + b²
  2. a² + 2ab + b²
  3. a² − 2ab + b²
  4. 2a + 2b
Jawab dekhein

B) a² + 2ab + b²

Q4. Line y = 3x + 2 ki slope kitni hai?

  1. 2
  2. 3
  3. 5
  4. 1/3
Jawab dekhein

B) 3

Q5. Triangle ke teeno angles ka majmua kitna hota hai?

  1. 90°
  2. 180°
  3. 270°
  4. 360°
Jawab dekhein

B) 180°

Q6. sin 30° ki value kitni hai?

  1. 1
  2. √3/2
  3. 1/2
  4. 0
Jawab dekhein

C) 1/2

Q7. 80 ka 25% kitna hai?

  1. 15
  2. 20
  3. 25
  4. 40
Jawab dekhein

B) 20

Q8. 2x + 5 = 15 mein x kitna hai?

  1. 4
  2. 5
  3. 10
  4. 7.5
Jawab dekhein

B) 5

Q9. Radius 7 wale circle ka area? (π = 22/7)

  1. 44
  2. 154
  3. 308
  4. 49
Jawab dekhein

B) 154

Q10. x² − 9 ki factorization kya hai?

  1. (x − 3)²
  2. (x − 3)(x + 3)
  3. (x − 9)(x + 1)
  4. x(x − 9)
Jawab dekhein

B) (x − 3)(x + 3)

Hissa 2: Mukhtasar sawal (3)

Sawal 1. x² − 7x + 10 = 0 hal karein.

Namoona jawab dekhein

Factors: (x − 2)(x − 5) = 0, is liye x = 2 ya x = 5.

Sawal 2. Base 10 aur height 6 wale triangle ka area nikalein.

Namoona jawab dekhein

Area = ½ × base × height = ½ × 10 × 6 = 30 square units.

Sawal 3. Pythagoras theorem likhein.

Namoona jawab dekhein

Right angle triangle mein hypotenuse ka square baqi do pehlon ke squares ke majmue ke barabar hota hai: c² = a² + b².
