Pakistan Movement MCQs with Answers

Class 9, 10 aur FSc · Likhne wali: Javeria Maqsood, Rehnuma · Aakhri update: 18 November 2026

Tehreek-e-Pakistan ke ye 12 MCQs Pakistan Studies ke imtihan ki tayyari ke liye hain. Pehle khud jawab sochein, phir "Jawab dekhein" dabayein.

1. All India Muslim League kab aur kahan qaim hui?

  • A) 1885, Bombay
  • B) 1906, Dhaka
  • C) 1930, Allahabad
  • D) 1940, Lahore
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: B) 1906, Dhaka
Muslim League 30 December 1906 ko Dhaka me qaim hui.

2. Qarardad-e-Pakistan (Lahore Resolution) kab pesh hui?

  • A) 23 March 1940
  • B) 14 August 1947
  • C) 23 March 1956
  • D) 11 September 1948
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: A) 23 March 1940
Ye qarardad Lahore me 23 March 1940 ko pesh hui.

3. Qarardad-e-Lahore kis ne pesh ki?

  • A) Allama Iqbal
  • B) Liaquat Ali Khan
  • C) Chaudhry Rehmat Ali
  • D) A. K. Fazlul Haq
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: D) A. K. Fazlul Haq
Qarardad A. K. Fazlul Haq (Sher-e-Bangal) ne pesh ki.

4. Allahabad ka khutba 1930 me kis ne diya?

  • A) Quaid-e-Azam
  • B) Sir Syed Ahmad Khan
  • C) Allama Muhammad Iqbal
  • D) Maulana Muhammad Ali Jauhar
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: C) Allama Muhammad Iqbal
Allama Iqbal ne 1930 me Muslim League ke Allahabad ijlas me alag Muslim riyasat ka tasawwur pesh kiya.

5. Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah kab aur kahan paida huye?

  • A) 25 December 1876, Lahore
  • B) 25 December 1876, Karachi
  • C) 11 September 1876, Karachi
  • D) 14 August 1876, Dhaka
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: B) 25 December 1876, Karachi
Quaid-e-Azam 25 December 1876 ko Karachi me paida huye.

6. Lucknow Pact kis saal hua?

  • A) 1916
  • B) 1906
  • C) 1930
  • D) 1947
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: A) 1916
Muslim League aur Congress ke darmiyan Lucknow Pact 1916 me hua.

7. Pakistan kab wujood me aaya?

  • A) 15 August 1947
  • B) 23 March 1940
  • C) 3 June 1947
  • D) 14 August 1947
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: D) 14 August 1947
Pakistan 14 August 1947 ko (27 Ramzan) wujood me aaya.

8. Jinnah ke 14 points kis saal pesh huye?

  • A) 1916
  • B) 1935
  • C) 1929
  • D) 1940
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: C) 1929
Quaid-e-Azam ne Nehru Report ke jawab me 1929 me 14 points pesh kiye.

9. Government of India Act kis saal manzoor hua?

  • A) 1909
  • B) 1935
  • C) 1919
  • D) 1947
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: B) 1935
Government of India Act 1935 me nafiz hua.

10. Pakistan ke pehle wazir-e-azam kaun the?

  • A) Liaquat Ali Khan
  • B) Khawaja Nazimuddin
  • C) Ghulam Muhammad
  • D) Huseyn Shaheed Suhrawardy
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: A) Liaquat Ali Khan
Liaquat Ali Khan Pakistan ke pehle wazir-e-azam the.

11. Cabinet Mission kis saal Hindustan aaya?

  • A) 1940
  • B) 1935
  • C) 1950
  • D) 1946
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: D) 1946
Cabinet Mission March 1946 me Hindustan aaya.

12. Hindustan aur Pakistan ki sarhad tay karne wale boundary commission ka sarbarah kaun tha?

  • A) Lord Mountbatten
  • B) Lord Wavell
  • C) Cyril Radcliffe
  • D) Stafford Cripps
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: C) Cyril Radcliffe
Radcliffe commission ka sarbarah Sir Cyril Radcliffe tha.
