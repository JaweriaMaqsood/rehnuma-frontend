1. When and where was the All India Muslim League founded?

  • A) 1885, Bombay
  • B) 1906, Dhaka
  • C) 1930, Allahabad
  • D) 1940, Lahore
Show answer

Correct answer: B) 1906, Dhaka
The Muslim League was founded in Dhaka on 30 December 1906.

1. All India Muslim League kab aur kahan qaim hui?

  • A) 1885, Bombay
  • B) 1906, Dhaka
  • C) 1930, Allahabad
  • D) 1940, Lahore
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: B) 1906, Dhaka
Muslim League 30 December 1906 ko Dhaka me qaim hui.

2. When was the Lahore Resolution presented?

  • A) 23 March 1940
  • B) 14 August 1947
  • C) 23 March 1956
  • D) 11 September 1948
Show answer

Correct answer: A) 23 March 1940
The resolution was presented in Lahore on 23 March 1940.

  • B) 14 August 1947
  • C) 23 March 1956
  • D) 11 September 1948

Sahi jawab: A) 23 March 1940

4. Who delivered the Allahabad Address in 1930?

  • A) Quaid-e-Azam
  • B) Sir Syed Ahmad Khan
  • C) Allama Muhammad Iqbal
  • D) Maulana Muhammad Ali Jauhar
Show answer

Correct answer: C) Allama Muhammad Iqbal

Allama Iqbal ne 1930 me Muslim League ke Allahabad ijlas me alag Muslim riyasat ka tasawwur pesh kiya.
