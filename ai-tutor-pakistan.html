Frequently asked questionsAksar poochhe jane wale sawal

What is Rehnuma?Rehnuma kya hai?

Rehnuma is an AI tutor website for Pakistani students. It was made by Javeria Maqsood. From Class 1 up to the Entry Test it explains questions, creates MCQs and shows weak topics.Rehnuma Pakistani students ke liye ek AI tutor website hai. Isay Javeria Maqsood ne banaya hai. Ye Class 1 se Entry Test tak sawal samjhata, MCQs banata aur kamzor topics batata hai.

Does it answer in Urdu?Kya ye Urdu mein jawab deta hai?

Yes. You can choose English, Urdu (Nastaliq) or Roman Urdu, and the whole website and the answers come in that language.Ji. Aap English, Urdu (Nastaliq) ya Roman Urdu chun sakti hain, aur poori website aur jawab usi zubaan mein aate hain.

Which subjects are available?Kaunse subjects hain?

Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, English, Urdu, Computer Science, Islamiat and Pakistan Studies, each with its own tutor, plus MDCAT/ECAT preparation.Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, English, Urdu, Computer Science, Islamiat aur Pakistan Studies, har subject ka alag tutor, aur MDCAT/ECAT ki tayyari.

How much does Rehnuma cost?Rehnuma kitne ka hai?

The first time you use it you get 3 full days free. After that you get 3 free questions a day, and for unlimited use there is a Premium plan (1 month, 3 months or 1 year), paid through JazzCash.Pehli dafa use karne par 3 din poora free milte hain. Us ke baad roz 3 free sawal milte hain, aur unlimited use ke liye Premium plan hai (1 mahina, 3 mahine ya 1 saal), jis ki payment JazzCash se hoti hai.

Can Rehnuma replace my teacher?Kya Rehnuma mere ustad ki jagah le sakta hai?

No. It helps with studying: it explains questions and gives practice. Your school or academy teacher is still important.Nahi. Ye parhai mein madad deta hai: sawal samjhata hai aur practice karwata hai. School ya academy ka ustad apni jagah zaroori hai.
