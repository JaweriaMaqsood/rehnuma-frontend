Newton's Three Laws of Motion: simple explanationNewton ke Teen Laws of Motion: aasan Roman Urdu mein

Newton's three laws are the foundation of Physics and appear in every exam from Class 9 to FSc. This guide explains all three laws with examples and numericals.Newton ke teen laws Physics ki buniyad hain aur Class 9 se FSc tak har imtihan mein aate hain. Is guide mein teeno laws misalon aur numericals ke sath samjhaye gaye hain.

First law: Law of InertiaPehla law: Law of Inertia

If an object is at rest it stays at rest, and if it is moving in a straight line at constant speed it keeps moving that way, unless a net (total) force acts on it.Agar koi cheez aaram ki halat mein hai to wo aaram mein hi rehti hai, aur agar wo ek seedhi line mein ek jaisi raftaar se chal rahi hai to usi tarah chalti rehti hai, jab tak us par koi net (kul) force na lage.

Example: when a bus brakes suddenly you lean forward, because your body wants to keep its earlier speed. This is called inertia. The greater the mass, the greater the inertia.Misal: bus achanak brake lagaye to aap aage ki taraf jhukte hain, kyunke aap ka jism pehle ki raftaar mein rehna chahta hai. Is ko inertia kehte hain. Jitna zyada mass, utna zyada inertia.

Second law: F = maDoosra law: F = ma

The net force on an object equals the product of its mass and acceleration.Kisi cheez par lagne wali net force us ke mass aur acceleration ke product ke barabar hoti hai.

F = m × a

  • F: force, unit newton (N)
  • m: mass, unit kilogram (kg)
  • a: acceleration, unit m/s²

So 1 N is the force that produces an acceleration of 1 m/s² in a mass of 1 kg. In terms of momentum: F = Δp / Δt.Yani 1 N wo force hai jo 1 kg mass mein 1 m/s² acceleration paida kare. Momentum ke lihaz se: F = Δp / Δt.

Solved numericalHal kiya hua numerical

Question: A net force of 3000 N acts on a 1200 kg car. What is its acceleration?Sawal: 1200 kg ki car par 3000 N ki net force lag rahi hai. Acceleration kya hogi?

Solution: a = F / m = 3000 / 1200 = 2.5 m/s².Hal: a = F / m = 3000 / 1200 = 2.5 m/s².

Question: How much force is needed to give a 5 kg block an acceleration of 2 m/s²?Sawal: 5 kg ke block ko 2 m/s² acceleration dene ke liye kitni force chahiye?

Solution: F = m × a = 5 × 2 = 10 N.Hal: F = m × a = 5 × 2 = 10 N.

Third law: Action and ReactionTeesra law: Action aur Reaction

Every action has an equal and opposite reaction.Har action ka barabar aur ulta reaction hota hai.

Example: you push the ground backward, the ground pushes you forward, and that is how you walk. A rocket throws gas downward and the gas pushes the rocket upward.Misal: aap zameen ko peeche dhakelte hain, zameen aap ko aage dhakelti hai, isi se aap chalte hain. Rocket gas ko neeche phenkta hai aur gas rocket ko oopar dhakelti hai.

Common mistake: action and reaction act on two different objects, so they do not cancel each other.Aam ghalti: action aur reaction do alag cheezon par lagte hain, is liye ye ek dusre ko cancel nahi karte.

Points to remember for the examImtihan ke liye yaad rakhne ki baatein

  • First law = inertia, second = F = ma, third = action-reaction.Pehla law = inertia, doosra = F = ma, teesra = action-reaction.
  • In a numerical, first write the given, then the formula, then the answer with units.Numerical mein pehle given likhein, phir formula, phir units ke sath jawab.
  • The unit of force is N, of mass is kg and of acceleration is m/s².Force ki unit N, mass ki kg, acceleration ki m/s² hoti hai.
