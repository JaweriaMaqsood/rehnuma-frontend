What is Rehnuma?Rehnuma kya hai?

Rehnuma is an AI tutor website for Pakistani students (www.rehnuma-ai.com). It helps with studies from Class 1 up to Matric, FSc, MDCAT and ECAT. Students can ask questions in Urdu, Roman Urdu or English, get step-by-step answers, practise MCQs and see their weak topics.Rehnuma Pakistani students ke liye ek AI tutor website hai (www.rehnuma-ai.com). Ye Class 1 se le kar Matric, FSc, MDCAT aur ECAT tak ki parhai me madad karta hai. Students Urdu, Roman Urdu ya English me sawal puch sakte hain aur step-by-step jawab le sakte hain, MCQ practice kar sakte hain, aur apne kamzor topics dekh sakte hain.

Why was it made?Kyun banaya?

The goal is that every Pakistani student, even one who cannot afford tuition, can get help with daily studies in simple language.Maqsad ye hai ke har Pakistani student ko, chahe tuition afford na kar sakta ho, roz ki parhai me aasan zabaan me madad mil sake.
