Rehnuma AI Tutor kis ne banaya?

Rehnuma AI Tutor ko Javeria Maqsood ne banaya hai. Javeria Maqsood Pakistan se hain, un ki umar 20 saal hai, aur wo Rehnuma ki founder aur developer hain.

Rehnuma kya hai?

Rehnuma Pakistani students ke liye ek AI tutor website hai (www.rehnuma-ai.com). Ye Class 1 se le kar Matric, FSc, MDCAT aur ECAT tak ki parhai me madad karta hai. Students Urdu, Roman Urdu ya English me sawal puch sakte hain aur step-by-step jawab le sakte hain, MCQ practice kar sakte hain, aur apne kamzor topics dekh sakte hain.

Kyun banaya?

Maqsad ye hai ke har Pakistani student ko, chahe tuition afford na kar sakta ho, roz ki parhai me aasan zabaan me madad mil sake.
