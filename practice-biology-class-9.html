Part 2: Short questions (3)Hissa 2: Mukhtasar sawal (3)

Question 1. Write two differences between a plant cell and an animal cell.Sawal 1. Plant cell aur animal cell mein do farq likhein.

Show sample answerNamoona jawab dekhein

A plant cell has a cell wall and chloroplasts, an animal cell does not. A plant cell has a large vacuole whereas an animal cell has small or few.Plant cell mein cell wall aur chloroplast hote hain, animal cell mein nahi. Plant cell mein bari vacuole hoti hai jabke animal cell mein chhoti ya kam.

Question 2. What is osmosis?Sawal 2. Osmosis kya hai?

Show sample answerNamoona jawab dekhein

Osmosis is the movement of water molecules through a semi-permeable membrane from a region of lower concentration to a region of higher concentration.Paani ke molecules ka kam concentration wale hisse se zyada concentration wale hisse ki taraf semi-permeable membrane se guzarna osmosis hai.

Question 3. What is the job of ribosomes?Sawal 3. Ribosomes ka kya kaam hai?

Show sample answerNamoona jawab dekhein

Ribosomes make proteins (protein synthesis).Ribosomes mein proteins banti hain (protein synthesis).
