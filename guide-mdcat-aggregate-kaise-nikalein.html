How to Calculate MDCAT and ECAT Aggregate: formula and exampleMDCAT aur ECAT Aggregate kaise nikalein: formula aur example

Written by: Javeria Maqsood, Rehnuma · Last updated: 13 November 2026Likhne wali: Javeria Maqsood, Rehnuma · Aakhri update: 13 November 2026

Admission merit is based on aggregate, which combines your Matric, FSc and entry test percentages according to their weightage. This guide shows the method for both MDCAT and ECAT with a solved example.Admission ki merit aggregate se banti hai, jo Matric, FSc aur entry test ke percentage ko un ke weightage se mila kar nikalta hai. Is guide mein MDCAT aur ECAT dono ka tareeqa ek hal kiye hue example ke sath hai.

What is aggregate?Aggregate kya hota hai

Aggregate is the weighted percentage combining your three results: Matric, FSc (or Intermediate) and the entry test. Each has a different weightage, so looking only at the test score is not enough.Aggregate aap ke teen result ka mila hua weighted percentage hai: Matric, FSc (ya Intermediate) aur entry test. Har ek ka weightage alag hota hai, isliye sirf test ke number dekhna kaafi nahi hota.

MDCAT aggregate formulaMDCAT aggregate ka formula

The usual weightage for MDCAT is:Aam tor par MDCAT ke liye weightage ye hota hai:

PartHissaWeightage
Matric10%
FSc40%
MDCAT test50%

Aggregate = (Matric % × 0.10) + (FSc % × 0.40) + (MDCAT % × 0.50)

To get the test percentage, divide your marks by the test's total marks and multiply by 100.Test ka percentage nikalne ke liye apne marks ko test ke total marks se divide kar ke 100 se zarb dein.

MDCAT solved exampleMDCAT ka hal kiya hua example

Suppose: Matric 92%, FSc 90%, MDCAT 81%.Farz karein: Matric 92%, FSc 90%, MDCAT 81%.

  • Matric: 92 × 0.10 = 9.2
  • FSc: 90 × 0.40 = 36
  • MDCAT: 81 × 0.50 = 40.5

Aggregate = 9.2 + 36 + 40.5 = 85.7%

ECAT aggregate formulaECAT aggregate ka formula

For UET's ECAT, the usual weightage is:UET ke ECAT ke liye aam tor par ye weightage use hota hai:

PartHissaWeightage
Matric17%
FSc50%
ECAT test33%

Aggregate = (Matric % × 0.17) + (FSc % × 0.50) + (ECAT % × 0.33)

Example: Matric 90%, FSc 85%, ECAT 300/400 = 75%. Aggregate = 15.3 + 42.5 + 24.75 = 82.55%.

An important cautionZaroori ehtiyat

The weightage can change every year and for each university (for example UET Peshawar or other engineering universities may have their own formula). Before admission, always check your university's official prospectus or notice. The numbers in this guide are for understanding and estimating.Weightage har saal aur har university ke liye badal sakta hai (jaise UET Peshawar ya doosri engineering universities ka apna formula ho sakta hai). Admission se pehle apni university ka official prospectus ya notice zaroor dekh lein. Is guide ke numbers samajhne aur andaza lagane ke liye hain.
