MDCAT aur ECAT Aggregate kaise nikalein: formula aur example

Likhne wali: Javeria Maqsood, Rehnuma · Aakhri update: 13 November 2026

Admission ki merit aggregate se banti hai, jo Matric, FSc aur entry test ke percentage ko un ke weightage se mila kar nikalta hai. Is guide mein MDCAT aur ECAT dono ka tareeqa ek hal kiye hue example ke sath hai.

Aggregate kya hota hai

Aggregate aap ke teen result ka mila hua weighted percentage hai: Matric, FSc (ya Intermediate) aur entry test. Har ek ka weightage alag hota hai, isliye sirf test ke number dekhna kaafi nahi hota.

MDCAT aggregate ka formula

Aam tor par MDCAT ke liye weightage ye hota hai:

HissaWeightage
Matric10%
FSc40%
MDCAT test50%

Aggregate = (Matric % × 0.10) + (FSc % × 0.40) + (MDCAT % × 0.50)

Test ka percentage nikalne ke liye apne marks ko test ke total marks se divide kar ke 100 se zarb dein.

MDCAT ka hal kiya hua example

Farz karein: Matric 92%, FSc 90%, MDCAT 81%.

  • Matric: 92 × 0.10 = 9.2
  • FSc: 90 × 0.40 = 36
  • MDCAT: 81 × 0.50 = 40.5

Aggregate = 9.2 + 36 + 40.5 = 85.7%

ECAT aggregate ka formula

UET ke ECAT ke liye aam tor par ye weightage use hota hai:

HissaWeightage
Matric17%
FSc50%
ECAT test33%

Aggregate = (Matric % × 0.17) + (FSc % × 0.50) + (ECAT % × 0.33)

Example: Matric 90%, FSc 85%, ECAT 300/400 = 75%. Aggregate = 15.3 + 42.5 + 24.75 = 82.55%.

Zaroori ehtiyat

Weightage har saal aur har university ke liye badal sakta hai (jaise UET Peshawar ya doosri engineering universities ka apna formula ho sakta hai). Admission se pehle apni university ka official prospectus ya notice zaroor dekh lein. Is guide ke numbers samajhne aur andaza lagane ke liye hain.
