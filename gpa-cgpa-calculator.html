How is GPA calculated?GPA kaise nikalte hain?

Multiply each course's grade point by its credit hours, add them up and divide by the total credit hours. For example A (4.0) with 3 credits and B (3.0) with 3 credits: (4×3 + 3×3) ÷ 6 = 3.5.Har course ke grade point ko credit hours se multiply karein, sab ko jod kar total credit hours se taqseem karein. Jaise A (4.0) 3 credit aur B (3.0) 3 credit: (4×3 + 3×3) ÷ 6 = 3.5.

What is the difference between CGPA and GPA?CGPA aur GPA me kya farq hai?

GPA is for one semester. CGPA is calculated by combining all semesters.GPA ek semester ka hota hai. CGPA tamam semesters ko mila kar nikalta hai.

Is every university's scale the same?Kya har university ka scale ek jaisa hota hai?

No. This calculator uses the standard 4.0 scale (A=4.0, A-=3.67, B+=3.33 and so on). Some universities differ slightly, so check your university's scale.Nahi. Is calculator me A=4.0, A-=3.67, B+=3.33 waghera standard 4.0 scale hai. Kuch universities me thora farq hota hai, apni university ka scale check karein.
