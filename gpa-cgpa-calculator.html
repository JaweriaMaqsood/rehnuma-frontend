GPA kaise nikalte hain?

Har course ke grade point ko credit hours se multiply karein, sab ko jod kar total credit hours se taqseem karein. Jaise A (4.0) 3 credit aur B (3.0) 3 credit: (4×3 + 3×3) ÷ 6 = 3.5.

CGPA aur GPA me kya farq hai?

GPA ek semester ka hota hai. CGPA tamam semesters ko mila kar nikalta hai.

Kya har university ka scale ek jaisa hota hai?

Nahi. Is calculator me A=4.0, A-=3.67, B+=3.33 waghera standard 4.0 scale hai. Kuch universities me thora farq hota hai, apni university ka scale check karein.
