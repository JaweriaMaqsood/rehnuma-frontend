Periodic Table MCQs with Answers

Class 9, 10, FSc aur MDCAT · Likhne wali: Javeria Maqsood, Rehnuma · Aakhri update: 18 November 2026

Periodic table ke ye 12 MCQs Chemistry ke imtihan aur MDCAT ki tayyari ke liye hain. Pehle khud jawab sochein, phir "Jawab dekhein" dabayein.

1. Modern periodic law ke mutabiq elements ki properties kis ka periodic function hain?

  • A) Atomic mass
  • B) Atomic number
  • C) Number of neutrons
  • D) Atomic radius
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: B) Atomic number
Moseley ke kaam ke baad periodic law atomic number par mabni hai. Mendeleev ne atomic mass use kiya tha.

2. Modern periodic table me kitne groups hain?

  • A) 18
  • B) 8
  • C) 16
  • D) 7
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: A) 18
Modern periodic table me 18 groups aur 7 periods hote hain.

3. Modern periodic table me kitne periods hain?

  • A) 8
  • B) 18
  • C) 6
  • D) 7
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: D) 7
Periods ki tadad 7 hai.

4. Sab se zyada electronegative element kaun sa hai?

  • A) Oxygen
  • B) Chlorine
  • C) Fluorine
  • D) Nitrogen
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: C) Fluorine
Fluorine ki electronegativity sab se zyada hai (Pauling scale par 4.0).

5. Noble gases kis group me aati hain?

  • A) Group 1
  • B) Group 18
  • C) Group 17
  • D) Group 2
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: B) Group 18
Helium, neon, argon wagaira group 18 me hain.

6. Period me left se right jane par atomic radius:

  • A) kam hota hai
  • B) barhta hai
  • C) badalta nahi
  • D) pehle barhta phir kam hota hai
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: A) kam hota hai
Nuclear charge barhne se electrons nucleus ki taraf zyada khinchte hain, isliye radius kam hota hai.

7. Group me oopar se neeche jane par ionization energy:

  • A) barhti hai
  • B) barabar rehti hai
  • C) pehle kam phir zyada hoti hai
  • D) kam hoti hai
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: D) kam hoti hai
Bahari electron nucleus se door hota hai, isliye usay nikalna aasan hota hai.

8. Halogens kis group ke elements hain?

  • A) Group 1
  • B) Group 2
  • C) Group 17
  • D) Group 18
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: C) Group 17
Fluorine, chlorine, bromine, iodine group 17 me hain.

9. Atomic number 11 wala element kaun sa hai?

  • A) Magnesium
  • B) Sodium
  • C) Neon
  • D) Potassium
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: B) Sodium
Sodium (Na) ka atomic number 11 hai.

10. In me se kaun sa noble gas hai?

  • A) Argon
  • B) Nitrogen
  • C) Oxygen
  • D) Chlorine
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: A) Argon
Argon (Ar) group 18 ka noble gas hai.

11. Mendeleev ne elements ko kis tarteeb me rakha tha?

  • A) Barhte hue atomic number ke mutabiq
  • B) Alphabet ki tarteeb me
  • C) Rang ke mutabiq
  • D) Barhte hue atomic mass ke mutabiq
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: D) Barhte hue atomic mass ke mutabiq
Mendeleev ka periodic table atomic mass par bana tha, jabke modern table atomic number par.

12. Group 1 me Li, Na, K, Rb me se sab se bara atomic radius kis ka hai?

  • A) Li
  • B) Na
  • C) Rb
  • D) K
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: C) Rb
Group me neeche jane par naye shells aate hain, isliye Rb sab se neeche hone ki wajah se sab se bara hai.
