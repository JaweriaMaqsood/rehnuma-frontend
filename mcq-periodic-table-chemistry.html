1. According to the modern periodic law, the properties of elements are a periodic function of their:

  • A) Atomic mass
  • B) Atomic number
  • C) Number of neutrons
  • D) Atomic radius
Show answer

Correct answer: B) Atomic number
After Moseley's work, the periodic law is based on atomic number. Mendeleev used atomic mass.

1. Modern periodic law ke mutabiq elements ki properties kis ka periodic function hain?

  • A) Atomic mass
  • B) Atomic number
  • C) Number of neutrons
  • D) Atomic radius
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: B) Atomic number
Moseley ke kaam ke baad periodic law atomic number par mabni hai. Mendeleev ne atomic mass use kiya tha.

2. How many groups are there in the modern periodic table?

  • A) 18
  • B) 8
  • C) 16
  • D) 7
Show answer

Correct answer: A) 18
The modern periodic table has 18 groups and 7 periods.

4. Which element is the most electronegative?

  • A) Oxygen
  • B) Chlorine
  • C) Fluorine
  • D) Nitrogen
Show answer

Correct answer: C) Fluorine
Fluorine has the highest electronegativity (4.0 on the Pauling scale).

4. Sab se zyada electronegative element kaun sa hai?

  • A) Oxygen
  • B) Chlorine
  • C) Fluorine
  • D) Nitrogen
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: C) Fluorine
Fluorine ki electronegativity sab se zyada hai (Pauling scale par 4.0).

5. The noble gases belong to which group?

  • A) Group 1
  • B) Group 18
  • C) Group 17
  • D) Group 2
Show answer

Correct answer: B) Group 18
Helium, neon, argon and the rest are in group 18.

5. Noble gases kis group me aati hain?

  • A) Group 1
  • B) Group 18
  • C) Group 17
  • D) Group 2
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: B) Group 18
Helium, neon, argon wagaira group 18 me hain.

6. Going from left to right across a period, atomic radius:

  • A) decreases
  • B) increases
  • C) stays the same
  • D) first increases then decreases
Show answer

Correct answer: A) decreases
As nuclear charge increases, electrons are pulled closer to the nucleus, so the radius decreases.

6. Period me left se right jane par atomic radius:

  • A) kam hota hai
  • B) barhta hai
  • C) badalta nahi
  • D) pehle barhta phir kam hota hai
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: A) kam hota hai
Nuclear charge barhne se electrons nucleus ki taraf zyada khinchte hain, isliye radius kam hota hai.

7. Going down a group, ionization energy:

  • A) increases
  • B) stays the same
  • C) first decreases then increases
  • D) decreases
Show answer

Correct answer: D) decreases
The outer electron is farther from the nucleus, so it is easier to remove.

7. Group me oopar se neeche jane par ionization energy:

  • A) barhti hai
  • B) barabar rehti hai
  • C) pehle kam phir zyada hoti hai
  • D) kam hoti hai
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: D) kam hoti hai
Bahari electron nucleus se door hota hai, isliye usay nikalna aasan hota hai.

8. Halogens are elements of which group?

  • A) Group 1
  • B) Group 2
  • C) Group 17
  • D) Group 18
Show answer

Correct answer: C) Group 17
Fluorine, chlorine, bromine and iodine are in group 17.

8. Halogens kis group ke elements hain?

  • A) Group 1
  • B) Group 2
  • C) Group 17
  • D) Group 18
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: C) Group 17
Fluorine, chlorine, bromine, iodine group 17 me hain.

  • A) Magnesium
  • B) Sodium

10. Which of these is a noble gas?

  • A) Argon
  • B) Nitrogen
  • C) Oxygen
  • D) Chlorine
Show answer

Correct answer: A) Argon
Argon (Ar) is a noble gas in group 18.

10. In me se kaun sa noble gas hai?

  • A) Argon
  • B) Nitrogen
  • C) Oxygen
  • D) Chlorine
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: A) Argon
Argon (Ar) group 18 ka noble gas hai.

11. In what order did Mendeleev arrange the elements?

  • A) By increasing atomic number
  • B) In alphabetical order
  • C) By colour
  • D) By increasing atomic mass
Show answer

Correct answer: D) By increasing atomic mass
Mendeleev's table was based on atomic mass, while the modern table is based on atomic number.

11. Mendeleev ne elements ko kis tarteeb me rakha tha?

  • A) Barhte hue atomic number ke mutabiq
  • B) Alphabet ki tarteeb me
  • C) Rang ke mutabiq
  • D) Barhte hue atomic mass ke mutabiq
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: D) Barhte hue atomic mass ke mutabiq
Mendeleev ka periodic table atomic mass par bana tha, jabke modern table atomic number par.

12. Among Li, Na, K and Rb in group 1, which has the largest atomic radius?

  • A) Li
  • B) Na
  • C) Rb
  • D) K
Show answer

Correct answer: C) Rb
New shells are added going down a group, so Rb, lowest in the group, is the largest.

12. Group 1 me Li, Na, K, Rb me se sab se bara atomic radius kis ka hai?

  • A) Li
  • B) Na
  • C) Rb
  • D) K
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: C) Rb
Group me neeche jane par naye shells aate hain, isliye Rb sab se neeche hone ki wajah se sab se bara hai.
