Active and Passive Voice: rules and examplesActive aur Passive Voice: rules aur examples

Written by: Javeria Maqsood, Rehnuma · Last updated: 2 November 2026Likhne wali: Javeria Maqsood, Rehnuma · Aakhri update: 2 November 2026

In active voice the doer (subject) does the action, and in passive voice the thing the action happens to (object) comes first.Active voice mein karne wala (subject) kaam karta hai, aur passive mein kaam jis par hua wo (object) pehle aata hai.

The passive rulePassive ka rule

Active: Subject + Verb + Object
Passive: Object + be-verb + V3 + by + Subject

The be-verb (is/am/are/was/were/been/being) changes according to the tense.Be-verb (is/am/are/was/were/been/being) tense ke hisab se badalta hai.

Tense-wise chart

Special casesKhaas sooratein

  • Command (imperative): "Close the door." → "Let the door be closed."Hukm (imperative): "Close the door." → "Let the door be closed."
  • Intransitive verbs (verbs with no object, like "sleep") cannot be made passive.Intransitive verbs (jin ka object nahi hota, jaise "sleep") ka passive nahi banta.
  • When the doer is unknown or unimportant, the "by..." part is dropped.Jab kaam karne wala maloom na ho ya ahem na ho, to "by..." hata dete hain.
