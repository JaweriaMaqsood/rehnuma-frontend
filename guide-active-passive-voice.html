Active aur Passive Voice: rules aur examples

Likhne wali: Javeria Maqsood, Rehnuma · Aakhri update: 2 November 2026

Active voice mein karne wala (subject) kaam karta hai, aur passive mein kaam jis par hua wo (object) pehle aata hai.

Passive ka rule

Active: Subject + Verb + Object
Passive: Object + be-verb + V3 + by + Subject

Be-verb (is/am/are/was/were/been/being) tense ke hisab se badalta hai.

Tense-wise chart

TenseActivePassive
Present SimpleHe writes a letter.A letter is written by him.
Present ContinuousThey are playing football.Football is being played by them.
Present PerfectHe has finished the work.The work has been finished by him.
Past SimpleShe cooked rice.Rice was cooked by her.
Past ContinuousShe was cooking rice.Rice was being cooked by her.
Past PerfectShe had cooked rice.Rice had been cooked by her.
Future SimpleI will help you.You will be helped by me.

Khaas sooratein

  • Hukm (imperative): "Close the door." → "Let the door be closed."
  • Intransitive verbs (jin ka object nahi hota, jaise "sleep") ka passive nahi banta.
  • Jab kaam karne wala maloom na ho ya ahem na ho, to "by..." hata dete hain.
