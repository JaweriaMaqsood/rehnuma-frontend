Photosynthesis Explained: step by step in simple wordsPhotosynthesis Meaning in Urdu: step by step Roman Urdu mein

Written by: Javeria Maqsood, Rehnuma · Last updated: 2 November 2026Likhne wali: Javeria Maqsood, Rehnuma · Aakhri update: 2 November 2026

Photosynthesis is the process in which plants use light energy to turn carbon dioxide and water into glucose and oxygen.Photosynthesis wo amal hai jis mein paude roshni ki energy se carbon dioxide aur paani ko glucose aur oxygen mein badalte hain.

Equation

6CO₂ + 6H₂O → (light, chlorophyll) → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂6CO₂ + 6H₂O → (roshni, chlorophyll) → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂

So 6 carbon dioxide and 6 water make 1 glucose and 6 oxygen.Yani 6 carbon dioxide aur 6 paani se 1 glucose aur 6 oxygen banti hai.

Where does it happen?Ye kahan hota hai

In the plant's chloroplast. Inside the chloroplast, the light reactions happen in the thylakoids (grana) and the Calvin cycle happens in the stroma. Chlorophyll absorbs light.Paude ke chloroplast mein. Chloroplast ke andar thylakoids (grana) mein light reactions aur stroma mein Calvin cycle hoti hai. Chlorophyll roshni ko jazb karta hai.

Stage 1: Light-dependent reactions

  • They happen in the thylakoid membrane.Thylakoid membrane mein hoti hain.
  • Light splits water (photolysis) and oxygen is released.Roshni se paani tootta hai (photolysis) aur oxygen bahar nikalti hai.
  • The energy is stored in ATP and NADPH.Energy ATP aur NADPH mein jama hoti hai.

Stage 2: Calvin cycle (light-independent)

  • It happens in the stroma.Stroma mein hoti hai.
  • The enzyme RuBisCO fixes CO₂.CO₂ ko enzyme RuBisCO fix karta hai.
  • With the help of ATP and NADPH, glucose is made (from triose phosphate).ATP aur NADPH ki madad se glucose (triose phosphate se) banta hai.

Limiting factors

The rate of photosynthesis depends on light intensity, CO₂ concentration and temperature. Whichever factor is lowest limits the rate.Photosynthesis ki raftaar in par depend karti hai: roshni ki shiddat, CO₂ ki miqdar aur temperature. Jo factor sab se kam ho wahi raftaar ko rokta hai.

A way to rememberYaad rakhne ka tareeqa

"In the light reactions water is split, in the Calvin cycle CO₂ is added." First light gives ATP/NADPH, then sugar is made from them."Light reactions mein paani tootta hai, Calvin cycle mein CO₂ judta hai." Pehle roshni ATP/NADPH deti hai, phir inhi se sugar banti hai.

What is the meaning of photosynthesis in Urdu?Photosynthesis ka Urdu mein matlab kya hai?

Photosynthesis is called ضیائی تالیف in Urdu. In it plants make their own food (glucose) from light, water and carbon dioxide and release oxygen.Photosynthesis ko Urdu mein ضیائی تالیف kehte hain. Is mein paude roshni, paani aur carbon dioxide se apni khoraak (glucose) banate hain aur oxygen nikalte hain.

What is the equation of photosynthesis?Photosynthesis ki equation kya hai?

6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ (in the presence of light and chlorophyll).6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ (roshni aur chlorophyll ki mojoodgi mein).

Where does the oxygen come from?Oxygen kahan se aati hai?

From the splitting of water (photolysis) in the light reactions.Light reactions mein paani ke tootne (photolysis) se.
