3 steps to identify a tenseTense pehchanne ke 3 qadam

  1. First see when the action happens: now (present), already happened (past), or yet to come (future).Pehle dekhein kaam kab ka hai: abhi (present), guzra hua (past), ya aane wala (future).
  2. Then see what state the action is in: ordinary (simple), ongoing (continuous), complete (perfect), or ongoing since a start point (perfect continuous).Phir dekhein kaam kis halat mein hai: aam (simple), jari (continuous), mukammal (perfect), ya shuru se jari (perfect continuous).
  3. Then apply the structure from the chart above.Phir upar ke chart se structure laga dein.

Common mistakesAam ghaltiyan

  • In simple present, he/she/it takes s/es on the verb: "He plays", "She goes".Simple present mein he/she/it ke sath verb mein s/es lagta hai: "He plays", "She goes".
  • When did is used with the past, the verb takes its first form (V1): "She did not play".Past ke sath did aaye to verb ka pehla form (V1) hota hai: "She did not play".
  • Difference between "has" and "have": has with he/she/it, have with the rest."Has" aur "have" ka farq: he/she/it ke sath has, baqi ke sath have.

How do you form tenses?Tense kaise banate hain?

First decide when the action happens (present, past or future), then see whether it is ongoing, complete or ordinary. Pick the right formula from the chart, for example Present Simple = Subject + V1 (s/es).Pehle faisla karein ke kaam kab hua (present, past ya future), phir dekhein ke kaam jaari hai, mukammal hai ya aam. How many tenses are there in English?English mein kitne tenses hote hain?

What is the formula for simple present?Simple present ka formula kya hai?
