Tense Banao: 12 English Tenses ka Chart, formulas aur examples

Likhne wali: Javeria Maqsood, Rehnuma · Aakhri update: 2 November 2026

Tenses ke 3 hisse hain: Present, Past aur Future, aur har ek ki 4 qismein. Neeche pura chart hai taake aap structure aur example ek hi jagah dekh saken.

Present Tenses

SimpleSubject + V1 (s/es)She plays cricket."khelti hai"
Continuousis/am/are + V-ingShe is playing cricket."khel rahi hai"
Perfecthas/have + V3She has played cricket."khel chuki hai"
Perfect Continuoushas/have been + V-ingShe has been playing for an hour."ek ghante se khel rahi hai"

Past Tenses

SimpleSubject + V2She played cricket."khelti thi / khela"
Continuouswas/were + V-ingShe was playing cricket."khel rahi thi"
Perfecthad + V3She had played cricket."khel chuki thi"
Perfect Continuoushad been + V-ingShe had been playing for an hour."ek ghante se khel rahi thi"

Future Tenses

Simplewill + V1She will play cricket."khelegi"
Continuouswill be + V-ingShe will be playing cricket."khel rahi hogi"
Perfectwill have + V3She will have played cricket."khel chuki hogi"
Perfect Continuouswill have been + V-ingShe will have been playing for an hour."ek ghante se khel rahi hogi"

Tense pehchanne ke 3 qadam

  1. Pehle dekhein kaam kab ka hai: abhi (present), guzra hua (past), ya aane wala (future).
  2. Phir dekhein kaam kis halat mein hai: aam (simple), jari (continuous), mukammal (perfect), ya shuru se jari (perfect continuous).
  3. Phir upar ke chart se structure laga dein.

Aam ghaltiyan

  • Simple present mein he/she/it ke sath verb mein s/es lagta hai: "He plays", "She goes".
  • Past ke sath did aaye to verb ka pehla form (V1) hota hai: "She did not play".
  • "Has" aur "have" ka farq: he/she/it ke sath has, baqi ke sath have.
