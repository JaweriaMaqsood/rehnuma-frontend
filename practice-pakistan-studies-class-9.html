Part 2: Short questions (3)Hissa 2: Mukhtasar sawal (3)

Question 1. Briefly write about the Two-Nation Theory.Sawal 1. Do qaumi nazariye ko mukhtasar likhein.

Show sample answerNamoona jawab dekhein

According to this theory, Muslims and Hindus in India are two separate nations in terms of religion, culture and way of life, so Muslims need a separate homeland.Is nazariye ke mutabiq Hindustan mein musalman aur hindu mazhab, saqafat aur rehan sehan ke lihaz se do alag qaumein hain, is liye musalmano ko alag watan chahiye.

Question 2. When and where was the Pakistan Resolution presented?Sawal 2. Qarardad-e-Pakistan kab aur kahan pesh hui?

Show sample answerNamoona jawab dekhein

On 23 March 1940, at the annual session of the Muslim League at Minto Park, Lahore (today's Iqbal Park).23 March 1940 ko Lahore ke Minto Park (aaj ka Iqbal Park) mein Muslim League ke salana ijlas mein.

Question 3. Write two importances of the Indus River.Sawal 3. Darya-e-Sindh ki do ahmiyatein likhein.

Show sample answerNamoona jawab dekhein

It provides water for agriculture and a source of hydropower.Zarai zameen ko paani aur pan bijli ka zariya.
