Hissa 1: MCQs (10)

Q1. Qarardad-e-Pakistan kis saal pesh hui?

  1. 1930
  2. 1940
  3. 1947
  4. 1956
Jawab dekhein

B) 1940

Q2. Pakistan ke bani kaun hain?

  1. Allama Iqbal
  2. Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah
  3. Liaquat Ali Khan
  4. Sir Syed Ahmad Khan
Jawab dekhein

B) Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah

Q3. Pakistan kab azad hua?

  1. 14 August 1947
  2. 23 March 1940
  3. 11 September 1948
  4. 6 September 1965
Jawab dekhein

A) 14 August 1947

Q4. Pakistan ke qaumi shayar kaun hain?

  1. Faiz Ahmad Faiz
  2. Allama Muhammad Iqbal
  3. Ahmad Faraz
  4. Mirza Ghalib
Jawab dekhein

B) Allama Muhammad Iqbal

Q5. Pakistan ka darul hukumat kaun sa hai?

  1. Karachi
  2. Lahore
  3. Islamabad
  4. Peshawar
Jawab dekhein

C) Islamabad

Q6. Pakistan ka sab se lamba darya kaun sa hai?

  1. Indus
  2. Jhelum
  3. Ravi
  4. Chenab
Jawab dekhein

A) Indus

Q7. Pakistan ki sab se unchi choti kaun si hai?

  1. Nanga Parbat
  2. K2
  3. Tirich Mir
  4. Rakaposhi
Jawab dekhein

B) K2

Q8. Khutba-e-Allahabad (1930) kis ne diya?

  1. Allama Iqbal
  2. Quaid-e-Azam
  3. Chaudhry Rahmat Ali
  4. Liaquat Ali Khan
Jawab dekhein

A) Allama Iqbal

Q9. Qarardad-e-Lahore kis ne pesh ki?

  1. A. K. Fazlul Huq
  2. Sir Syed Ahmad Khan
  3. Liaquat Ali Khan
  4. Maulana Muhammad Ali Jauhar
Jawab dekhein

A) A. K. Fazlul Huq

Q10. Pakistan ka pehla aaeen kis saal nafiz hua?

  1. 1947
  2. 1956
  3. 1962
  4. 1973
Jawab dekhein

B) 1956

Hissa 2: Mukhtasar sawal (3)

Sawal 1. Do qaumi nazariye ko mukhtasar likhein.

Namoona jawab dekhein

Is nazariye ke mutabiq Hindustan mein musalman aur hindu mazhab, saqafat aur rehan sehan ke lihaz se do alag qaumein hain, is liye musalmano ko alag watan chahiye.

Sawal 2. Qarardad-e-Pakistan kab aur kahan pesh hui?

Namoona jawab dekhein

23 March 1940 ko Lahore ke Minto Park (aaj ka Iqbal Park) mein Muslim League ke salana ijlas mein.

Sawal 3. Darya-e-Sindh ki do ahmiyatein likhein.

Namoona jawab dekhein

Zarai zameen ko paani aur pan bijli ka zariya.
