Kya ye calculator free hai?

Degree aur radian me kaise badlein?

Calculator ke upar wale button "DEG" par click karein, wo "RAD" ho jata hai. Trigonometry (sin, cos, tan) ke jawab usi mode ke mutabiq aate hain.

Kya ye board imtihan me use ho sakta hai?

Nahi. Imtihan me sirf wahi calculator allowed hota hai jo board ya test ka notice batata hai. Ye calculator practice ke liye hai.

Kya sin, cos, tan ka sahi jawab milta hai?

Ji. Mode (degree ya radian) sahi chunein. Bohat chhoti values (jaise sin 180) me computer ki wajah se 0.0000000001 jaisa bohat chhota farq aa sakta hai, ye round kar diya jata hai.
