UET Peshawar ka aggregate formula kya hai?

Online sources me formula ek jaisa nahi likha. Ek jagah FSc 70% aur test 30% likha hai, dusri jagah FSc 30% aur test 70%. Is liye is calculator me dono option aur khud likhne ka option hai. Sahi aur is saal ka formula UET Peshawar ke official prospectus ya notice se check karein.

UET Peshawar me Matric ke marks lagte hain?

Kuch sources me Matric ka zikr nahi hai. Is calculator me Matric ka weightage 0 rakha gaya hai; agar aap ki prospectus me Matric ka weightage hai to usay badal lein.

Entry test ke total marks kitne hote hain?

Alag sources me alag likha hai (jaise 200 ya 400). Jo is saal ke paper ke total marks hon wahi likhein.

Kya ye final merit hai?

Nahi, ye sirf andaza hai. Asal merit list ke liye UET Peshawar ka official notice dekhein.
