Organelles and their jobsOrganelles aur unka kaam

  • Nucleus: holds DNA and controls the cell's activities.Nucleus: DNA rakhta hai aur cell ke kaam control karta hai.
  • Mitochondria: makes ATP (energy) through cellular respiration. It has two membranes and its own DNA.Mitochondria: cellular respiration se ATP (energy) banata hai. Is ki do jhilliyan hoti hain aur apna DNA bhi hota hai.
  • Ribosome: makes protein (protein synthesis).Ribosome: protein banata hai (protein synthesis).
  • Rough ER: has ribosomes on it and helps make and transport proteins.Rough ER: is par ribosomes hote hain, proteins banane aur lane le jane mein madad.
  • Smooth ER: makes lipids and detoxifies poisons.Smooth ER: lipids banata hai aur zehar ko be-asar karta hai.
  • Golgi apparatus: modifies, packs and ships proteins.Golgi apparatus: proteins ko tabdeel, pack aur bhejta hai.
  • Lysosome: holds digestive enzymes and breaks down waste and old parts.Lysosome: hazma karne wale enzymes rakhta hai, kachra aur purane hisse todta hai.
  • Plasma membrane: controls what enters and leaves the cell.Plasma membrane: cell ke andar aane aur jaane wali cheezon ko control karti hai.

What is found only in plant cellsSirf plant cell mein kya hota hai

  • Cell wall: made of cellulose in plants; it gives the cell a firm shape.Cell wall: plants mein cellulose ki bani hoti hai aur cell ko sakht shakl deti hai.
  • Chloroplast: the site of photosynthesis; it contains chlorophyll.Chloroplast: photosynthesis ki jagah, isme chlorophyll hota hai.
  • Large central vacuole: stores water and dissolved substances.Bari central vacuole: paani aur ghulay hue cheezein jama karti hai.

Prokaryotes and EukaryotesProkaryote aur Eukaryote

  • Prokaryotes (like bacteria): no nucleus, DNA lies in the cytoplasm, small ribosomes (70S).Prokaryote (jaise bacteria): nucleus nahi hota, DNA cytoplasm mein hota hai, ribosomes chhote (70S).
  • Eukaryotes (plants, animals, fungi): a true nucleus, larger ribosomes (80S) and membrane-bound organelles.Eukaryote (plants, animals, fungi): asli nucleus hota hai, ribosomes bare (80S) aur membrane wale organelles hote hain.

Points to remember for the examImtihan ke liye yaad rakhne ki baatein

  • Mitochondria = ATP, "powerhouse".
  • Ribosome = protein, Golgi = pack and ship, lysosome = digestion.Ribosome = protein, Golgi = pack aur bhejna, lysosome = hazma.
  • Cell wall and chloroplast are found only in plants.Cell wall aur chloroplast sirf plant mein.
