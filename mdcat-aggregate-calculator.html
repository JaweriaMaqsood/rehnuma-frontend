MDCAT aggregate ka formula kya hai?

Aam tor par use hone wala formula: Matric % × 0.10 + FSc % × 0.40 + MDCAT % × 0.50. Ye formula har saal PMDC/university ke prospectus ke mutabiq badal sakta hai, aakhri tasdeeq official notice se karein.

MDCAT ke total marks kitne hote hain?

Is calculator me 180 default hai (ya jo is saal ka paper ho). Pichle saloon me 200 ya 210 bhi rahe hain. Isi liye aap total khud badal sakti hain.

Kya ye final merit hai?

Nahi. Ye sirf andaza hai. Asal merit list university/UHS ya PMDC ke official notice ke mutabiq banti hai, aur NUMS, AKU jaisi jagahon ka formula alag ho sakta hai.

Matric ya FSc ke total marks kitne daalein?

Apni result card ke mutabiq. Aam tor par Punjab me Matric 1200 aur FSc 1100 hote hain, federal board me alag ho sakte hain.
