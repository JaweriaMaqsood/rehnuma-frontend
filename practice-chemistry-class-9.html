Part 2: Short questions (3)Hissa 2: Mukhtasar sawal (3)

Question 1. What are isotopes?Sawal 1. Isotopes kya hote hain?

Show sample answerNamoona jawab dekhein

Atoms of the same element that have the same atomic number but different mass numbers (a different number of neutrons), such as carbon-12 and carbon-14.Ek hi element ke wo atoms jin ka atomic number barabar lekin mass number alag ho (neutrons ki tadad alag), jaise carbon-12 aur carbon-14.

Question 2. Write the difference between ionic and covalent bonds.Sawal 2. Ionic aur covalent bond mein farq likhein.

Show sample answerNamoona jawab dekhein

In an ionic bond, electrons are transferred from one atom to another and ions form. In a covalent bond, atoms share electrons.Ionic bond mein electrons ek atom se doosre ko muntaqil hote hain aur ions banti hain. Covalent bond mein atoms electrons share karte hain.

Question 3. How many moles are in 36 g of water? (H = 1, O = 16)Sawal 3. 36 g paani mein kitne moles hain? (H = 1, O = 16)

Show sample answerNamoona jawab dekhein

Molar mass of H₂O = 18 g/mol. Moles = 36 ÷ 18 = 2 mol.Molar mass H₂O = 18 g/mol. Moles = 36 ÷ 18 = 2 mol.
