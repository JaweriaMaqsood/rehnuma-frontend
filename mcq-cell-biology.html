1. Which organelle is called the "powerhouse" of the cell?

  • A) Ribosome
  • B) Mitochondria
  • C) Golgi apparatus
  • D) Lysosome
Show answer

Correct answer: B) Mitochondria
Mitochondria make ATP (energy) through cellular respiration.

1. Cell ka "powerhouse" kise kaha jata hai?

  • A) Ribosome
  • B) Mitochondria
  • C) Golgi apparatus
  • D) Lysosome
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: B) Mitochondria
Mitochondria cellular respiration se ATP (energy) banate hain.

2. Where does protein synthesis take place?

  • A) Ribosome
  • B) Lysosome
  • C) Vacuole
  • D) Centriole
Show answer

Correct answer: A) Ribosome
Amino acids are joined into proteins on ribosomes.

2. Protein synthesis kahan hoti hai?

  • A) Ribosome
  • B) Lysosome
  • C) Vacuole
  • D) Centriole
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: A) Ribosome
Ribosomes par amino acids jor kar protein banaye jate hain.

3. What is the cell wall of a plant cell made of?

  • A) Chitin
  • B) Peptidoglycan
  • C) Protein
  • D) Cellulose
Show answer

Correct answer: D) Cellulose
Plant cell walls are made of cellulose. Fungi have chitin.

3. Plant cell ki cell wall kis cheez ki bani hoti hai?

  • A) Chitin
  • B) Peptidoglycan
  • C) Protein
  • D) Cellulose
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: D) Cellulose
Plants ki cell wall cellulose ki hoti hai. Fungi me chitin hoti hai.

4. In which organelle does photosynthesis take place?

  • A) Mitochondria
  • B) Nucleus
  • C) Chloroplast
  • D) Golgi apparatus
Show answer

Correct answer: C) Chloroplast
The chloroplast contains chlorophyll, and photosynthesis happens there.

4. Photosynthesis cell ke kis organelle me hoti hai?

  • A) Mitochondria
  • B) Nucleus
  • C) Chloroplast
  • D) Golgi apparatus
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: C) Chloroplast
Chloroplast me chlorophyll hota hai aur photosynthesis wahin hoti hai.

5. Which part controls the activities of the cell?

  • A) Ribosome
  • B) Nucleus
  • C) Cell membrane
  • D) Vacuole
Show answer

Correct answer: B) Nucleus
The nucleus contains DNA, which controls the cell's activities.

5. Cell ke kaamon ko kaun control karta hai?

  • A) Ribosome
  • B) Nucleus
  • C) Cell membrane
  • D) Vacuole
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: B) Nucleus
Nucleus me DNA hota hai jo cell ke kaam control karta hai.

6. What is absent in prokaryotic cells?

  • A) A membrane-bound nucleus
  • B) Ribosomes
  • C) DNA
  • D) Cell membrane
Show answer

Correct answer: A) A membrane-bound nucleus
Prokaryotes have no membrane-bound nucleus. Their DNA lies in the cytoplasm.

6. Prokaryotic cells me kya nahi hota?

  • A) Membrane wala asli nucleus
  • B) Ribosome
  • C) DNA
  • D) Cell membrane
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: A) Membrane wala asli nucleus
Prokaryotes me nucleus ghilaf wala nahi hota, DNA cytoplasm me hota hai.

7. Which organelle contains digestive enzymes?

  • A) Ribosome
  • B) Chloroplast
  • C) Nucleolus
  • D) Lysosome
Show answer

Correct answer: D) Lysosome
Lysosomes contain enzymes that digest materials.

7. Digestive enzymes kis organelle me hote hain?

  • A) Ribosome
  • B) Chloroplast
  • C) Nucleolus
  • D) Lysosome
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: D) Lysosome
Lysosomes me hazma karne wale enzymes hote hain.

8. Who proposed the fluid mosaic model of the cell membrane?

  • A) Robert Hooke
  • B) Schleiden and Schwann
  • C) Singer and Nicolson
  • D) Watson and Crick
Show answer

Correct answer: C) Singer and Nicolson
The fluid mosaic model was proposed by Singer and Nicolson in 1972.

8. Cell membrane ka fluid mosaic model kis ne pesh kiya?

  • A) Robert Hooke
  • B) Schleiden aur Schwann
  • C) Singer aur Nicolson
  • D) Watson aur Crick
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: C) Singer aur Nicolson
Fluid mosaic model 1972 me Singer aur Nicolson ne diya.

9. Why is rough ER called "rough"?

  • A) It contains DNA
  • B) It has ribosomes on its surface
  • C) It contains chlorophyll
  • D) It has no membrane
Show answer

Correct answer: B) It has ribosomes on its surface
The ribosomes make its surface look rough.

9. Rough ER ko rough kyun kaha jata hai?

  • A) Is me DNA hota hai
  • B) Is ki satah par ribosomes hote hain
  • C) Is me chlorophyll hota hai
  • D) Is ki jhilli nahi hoti
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: B) Is ki satah par ribosomes hote hain
Ribosomes ki wajah se satah khurdari lagti hai.

10. Who first observed and named the cell?

  • A) Robert Hooke
  • B) Louis Pasteur
  • C) Gregor Mendel
  • D) Charles Darwin
Show answer

Correct answer: A) Robert Hooke
Robert Hooke observed cells in a thin slice of cork in 1665.

10. Cell ko sab se pehle kis ne dekha aur naam diya?

  • A) Robert Hooke
  • B) Louis Pasteur
  • C) Gregor Mendel
  • D) Charles Darwin
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: A) Robert Hooke
Robert Hooke ne 1665 me cork ke tukde me cells dekhe.

11. Which organelle modifies, packs and sends out proteins?

  • A) Mitochondria
  • B) Lysosome
  • C) Nucleolus
  • D) Golgi apparatus
Show answer

Correct answer: D) Golgi apparatus
The Golgi apparatus modifies, packs and sends proteins inside or outside the cell.

11. Proteins ko tabdeel kar ke pack aur bhejne ka kaam kis organelle ka hai?

  • A) Mitochondria
  • B) Lysosome
  • C) Nucleolus
  • D) Golgi apparatus
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: D) Golgi apparatus
Golgi apparatus proteins ko modify, pack aur cell ke andar ya bahar bhejta hai.

12. Which of these is NOT found in an animal cell?

  • A) Nucleus
  • B) Mitochondria
  • C) Cell wall
  • D) Plasma membrane
Show answer

Correct answer: C) Cell wall
Animal cells have no cell wall. It is found in plants, fungi and bacteria.

12. In me se kaun sa animal cell me nahi hota?

  • A) Nucleus
  • B) Mitochondria
  • C) Cell wall
  • D) Plasma membrane
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: C) Cell wall
Animal cell me cell wall nahi hoti, ye plant, fungi aur bacteria me hoti hai.
