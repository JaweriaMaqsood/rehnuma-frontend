Cell Biology MCQs with Answers

Class 9, FSc aur MDCAT · Likhne wali: Javeria Maqsood, Rehnuma · Aakhri update: 18 November 2026

Cell Biology ke ye 12 MCQs Biology ke imtihan aur MDCAT ki tayyari ke liye hain. Pehle khud jawab sochein, phir "Jawab dekhein" dabayein.

1. Cell ka "powerhouse" kise kaha jata hai?

  • A) Ribosome
  • B) Mitochondria
  • C) Golgi apparatus
  • D) Lysosome
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: B) Mitochondria
Mitochondria cellular respiration se ATP (energy) banate hain.

2. Protein synthesis kahan hoti hai?

  • A) Ribosome
  • B) Lysosome
  • C) Vacuole
  • D) Centriole
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: A) Ribosome
Ribosomes par amino acids jor kar protein banaye jate hain.

3. Plant cell ki cell wall kis cheez ki bani hoti hai?

  • A) Chitin
  • B) Peptidoglycan
  • C) Protein
  • D) Cellulose
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: D) Cellulose
Plants ki cell wall cellulose ki hoti hai. Fungi me chitin hoti hai.

4. Photosynthesis cell ke kis organelle me hoti hai?

  • A) Mitochondria
  • B) Nucleus
  • C) Chloroplast
  • D) Golgi apparatus
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: C) Chloroplast
Chloroplast me chlorophyll hota hai aur photosynthesis wahin hoti hai.

5. Cell ke kaamon ko kaun control karta hai?

  • A) Ribosome
  • B) Nucleus
  • C) Cell membrane
  • D) Vacuole
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: B) Nucleus
Nucleus me DNA hota hai jo cell ke kaam control karta hai.

6. Prokaryotic cells me kya nahi hota?

  • A) Membrane wala asli nucleus
  • B) Ribosome
  • C) DNA
  • D) Cell membrane
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: A) Membrane wala asli nucleus
Prokaryotes me nucleus ghilaf wala nahi hota, DNA cytoplasm me hota hai.

7. Digestive enzymes kis organelle me hote hain?

  • A) Ribosome
  • B) Chloroplast
  • C) Nucleolus
  • D) Lysosome
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: D) Lysosome
Lysosomes me hazma karne wale enzymes hote hain.

8. Cell membrane ka fluid mosaic model kis ne pesh kiya?

  • A) Robert Hooke
  • B) Schleiden aur Schwann
  • C) Singer aur Nicolson
  • D) Watson aur Crick
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: C) Singer aur Nicolson
Fluid mosaic model 1972 me Singer aur Nicolson ne diya.

9. Rough ER ko rough kyun kaha jata hai?

  • A) Is me DNA hota hai
  • B) Is ki satah par ribosomes hote hain
  • C) Is me chlorophyll hota hai
  • D) Is ki jhilli nahi hoti
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: B) Is ki satah par ribosomes hote hain
Ribosomes ki wajah se satah khurdari lagti hai.

10. Cell ko sab se pehle kis ne dekha aur naam diya?

  • A) Robert Hooke
  • B) Louis Pasteur
  • C) Gregor Mendel
  • D) Charles Darwin
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: A) Robert Hooke
Robert Hooke ne 1665 me cork ke tukde me cells dekhe.

11. Proteins ko tabdeel kar ke pack aur bhejne ka kaam kis organelle ka hai?

  • A) Mitochondria
  • B) Lysosome
  • C) Nucleolus
  • D) Golgi apparatus
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: D) Golgi apparatus
Golgi apparatus proteins ko modify, pack aur cell ke andar ya bahar bhejta hai.

12. In me se kaun sa animal cell me nahi hota?

  • A) Nucleus
  • B) Mitochondria
  • C) Cell wall
  • D) Plasma membrane
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: C) Cell wall
Animal cell me cell wall nahi hoti, ye plant, fungi aur bacteria me hoti hai.
