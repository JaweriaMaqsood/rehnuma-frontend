Frequently asked questionsAksar poochhe jane wale sawal

What is the ECAT aggregate formula?ECAT aggregate ka formula kya hai?

Usually for UET Lahore: Matric % × 0.17 + FSc % × 0.50 + ECAT % × 0.33 (out of 100). Other engineering universities (like Taxila, Peshawar, NUST) may have a different weightage.Aam tor par UET Lahore ke liye: Matric % × 0.17 + FSc % × 0.50 + ECAT % × 0.33 (total 100 me). Dusri engineering universities (jaise Taxila, Peshawar, NUST) ka weightage alag ho sakta hai.

How many total marks does ECAT have?ECAT ke total marks kitne hote hain?

ECAT is usually out of 400 marks (100 MCQs, 4 marks each). Enter the total for this year's paper.Aam tor par ECAT 400 marks ka hota hai (100 MCQs, har ek ke 4). Jo is saal ka paper ho wahi total daalein.

Is this the final merit?Kya ye final merit hai?

No, this is only an estimate. For the actual merit list see your university's official notice.Nahi, ye sirf andaza hai. Asal merit list ke liye apni university ka official notice dekhein.
