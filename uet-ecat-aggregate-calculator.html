ECAT aggregate ka formula kya hai?

Aam tor par UET Lahore ke liye: Matric % × 0.17 + FSc % × 0.50 + ECAT % × 0.33 (total 100 me). Dusri engineering universities (jaise Taxila, Peshawar, NUST) ka weightage alag ho sakta hai.

ECAT ke total marks kitne hote hain?

Aam tor par ECAT 400 marks ka hota hai (100 MCQs, har ek ke 4). Jo is saal ka paper ho wahi total daalein.

Kya ye final merit hai?

Nahi, ye sirf andaza hai. Asal merit list ke liye apni university ka official notice dekhein.
