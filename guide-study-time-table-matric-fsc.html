Pomodoro tareeqa

25 minute dhyan se parhein, phir 5 minute aaram. 4 round ke baad 15 se 20 minute ka lamba break lein. Is dauran phone se door rahein.

Revision ka asaan usool

  • Naya topic parhne ke 24 ghante ke andar ek dafa dohrayein.
  • Phir 1 hafte aur 1 mahine baad dobara dekhein.
  • Sirf parhne ke bajaye khud se sawal poochein aur likh kar jawab dein.

Imtihan se pehle

  • Pichle saalon ke papers apne board ke mutabiq hal karein.
  • Numericals aur formulas ki ek chhoti copy banayein.
  • 7 se 8 ghante ki neend lein aur pani peete rahein.
