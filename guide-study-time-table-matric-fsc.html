The Pomodoro methodPomodoro tareeqa

Study with focus for 25 minutes, then rest for 5 minutes. After 4 rounds take a longer break of 15 to 20 minutes. Keep your phone away during this time.25 minute dhyan se parhein, phir 5 minute aaram. 4 round ke baad 15 se 20 minute ka lamba break lein. Is dauran phone se door rahein.

A simple rule for revisionRevision ka asaan usool

  • Revise a new topic once within 24 hours of learning it.Naya topic parhne ke 24 ghante ke andar ek dafa dohrayein.
  • Then look at it again after 1 week and 1 month.Phir 1 hafte aur 1 mahine baad dobara dekhein.
  • Instead of just reading, ask yourself questions and write the answers.Sirf parhne ke bajaye khud se sawal poochein aur likh kar jawab dein.

Before the examImtihan se pehle

  • Solve past papers for your board.Pichle saalon ke papers apne board ke mutabiq hal karein.
  • Make a small copy of numericals and formulas.Numericals aur formulas ki ek chhoti copy banayein.
  • Sleep 7 to 8 hours and keep drinking water.7 se 8 ghante ki neend lein aur pani peete rahein.
