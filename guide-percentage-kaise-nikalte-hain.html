Percentage kaise nikalte hain: formula aur examples

Likhne wali: Javeria Maqsood, Rehnuma · Aakhri update: 13 November 2026

Percentage Maths ka sab se zyada istemal hone wala topic hai, marks se le kar discount tak. Is guide mein chaar aam qisam ke sawal hal kiye gaye hain.

Percentage ka bunyadi formula

Percentage = (Hasil kiye hue marks ÷ Kul marks) × 100

Percent ka matlab hai "har sau mein se". 50% ka matlab 100 mein se 50.

Marks ka percentage

Sawal: 1100 mein se 850 marks aaye. Percentage kya hui?

Hal: (850 ÷ 1100) × 100 = 77.27%

Ulta sawal: 1100 mein se 60% ke liye kitne marks chahiye?

Hal: 1100 × 60 ÷ 100 = 660 marks

Percent increase aur decrease

Change % = ((Nayi value − Purani value) ÷ Purani value) × 100

Sawal: Marks 40 se 50 ho gaye. Kitna percent izafa hua?

Hal: (50 − 40) ÷ 40 × 100 = 25% izafa.

Dhyan rakhein: hamesha purani value se divide karte hain.

Discount ka sawal

Sawal: 2000 rupay ki cheez par 15% discount hai. Nayi qeemat kya hogi?

Hal: Discount = 2000 × 15 ÷ 100 = 300. Nayi qeemat = 2000 − 300 = 1700 rupay.

Kul value nikalna

Sawal: Ek adad ka 30% 45 hai. Adad kya hai?

Hal: Adad = 45 × 100 ÷ 30 = 150.
