Hissa 1: MCQs (10)

Q1. "Go" ka past tense kya hai?

  1. Goed
  2. Gone
  3. Went
  4. Going
Jawab dekhein

C) Went

Q2. "Child" ka plural kya hai?

  1. Childs
  2. Children
  3. Childes
  4. Childrens
Jawab dekhein

B) Children

Q3. "Happy" ka synonym kaun sa hai?

  1. Sad
  2. Joyful
  3. Angry
  4. Tired
Jawab dekhein

B) Joyful

Q4. "Ancient" ka antonym kaun sa hai?

  1. Old
  2. Modern
  3. Historic
  4. Past
Jawab dekhein

B) Modern

Q5. She ___ to school every day.

  1. go
  2. goes
  3. going
  4. gone
Jawab dekhein

B) goes

Q6. "He wrote a letter." ka passive kya hai?

  1. A letter is written by him.
  2. A letter was written by him.
  3. A letter has written by him.
  4. A letter wrote him.
Jawab dekhein

B) A letter was written by him.

Q7. ___ apple a day keeps the doctor away.

  1. A
  2. An
  3. The
  4. No article
Jawab dekhein

B) An

Q8. Sahi spelling kaun si hai?

  1. Recieve
  2. Receive
  3. Receeve
  4. Recive
Jawab dekhein

B) Receive

Q9. The book is ___ the table.

  1. on
  2. at
  3. by
  4. of
Jawab dekhein

A) on

Q10. "Quickly" kis part of speech ka lafz hai?

  1. Adjective
  2. Noun
  3. Adverb
  4. Verb
Jawab dekhein

C) Adverb

Hissa 2: Mukhtasar sawal (3)

Sawal 1. Ek din ki chutti ke liye principal ko application likhein (khaka).

Namoona jawab dekhein

Subject, Respected Sir, wajah (jaise bimari), ek din ki chutti ki darkhwast, shukriya aur aap ka naam, class aur tareekh.

Sawal 2. "although" se ek jumla banayein.

Namoona jawab dekhein

Although it was raining, we went to school.

Sawal 3. Indirect speech mein badlein: He said, "I am tired."

Namoona jawab dekhein

He said that he was tired.
