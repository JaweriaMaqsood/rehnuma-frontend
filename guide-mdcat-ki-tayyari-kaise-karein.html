How to Prepare for MDCAT: a clear study planMDCAT ki Tayyari Kaise Karein: ek saaf study plan

Written by: Javeria Maqsood, Rehnuma · Last updated: 2 November 2026Likhne wali: Javeria Maqsood, Rehnuma · Aakhri update: 2 November 2026

This guide is a general study plan. Always check the official MDCAT syllabus, paper pattern and dates on the official PMDC website, because they can change every year.Ye guide ek aam study plan hai. MDCAT ka official syllabus, paper ka pattern aur tareekhein hamesha PMDC ki official website se dekhein, kyunke ye har saal badal sakti hain.

1) First look at the syllabus1) Pehle syllabus dekhein

Download the official syllabus and make a subject-wise list of topics (Biology, Chemistry, Physics, English and Logical Reasoning). Put a tick box next to every topic.Official syllabus download kar ke subject-wise topics ki list banayein (Biology, Chemistry, Physics, English aur Logical Reasoning). Har topic ke samne tick ka dabba rakhein.

2) First concept, then MCQs2) Pehle concept, phir MCQ

Understand a topic before you memorise it. Once the concept is clear, do the MCQs of that topic. This way you don't have to memorise MCQ answers, you work them out.Rate (ratta) lagane se pehle topic samjhein. Jab concept samajh aa jaye tab us topic ke MCQs karein. Is tareeqe se MCQ ke jawab yaad nahi karne parte, aap un ko nikal lete hain.

3) A small daily plan3) Roz ka chhota plan

  • Split your study across 2 to 3 subjects, not just one subject every day.2 se 3 subjects mein baant kar parhein, roz ek hi subject nahi.
  • Make small blocks of 25 minutes of study and 5 minutes of rest (Pomodoro).25 minute parhai aur 5 minute aaram (Pomodoro) ke chhote blocks banayein.
  • At the end of the day, do 10 MCQs on the topic you studied today.Din ke aakhir mein 10 MCQs us topic ke karein jo aaj parha.

4) A register of weak topics4) Kamzor topics ka register

Write the topic of every wrong MCQ in a copy. Once a week, spend a day only on these weak topics. Your weak topics also show in the Progress section of Rehnuma.Har ghalat MCQ ka topic ek copy mein likhein. Hafte mein ek din sirf in kamzor topics ko dobara karein. Rehnuma ke Progress section mein bhi aap ke kamzor topics nazar aate hain.

5) Revision and mock tests5) Revision aur mock tests

  • Revisit each topic after 1 day, 1 week and 1 month.Har topic 1 din, 1 hafte aur 1 mahine baad dobara dekhein.
  • Before the exam, take full mock tests under timed supervision.Imtihan se pehle waqt nigrani ke sath poore mock tests dein.
  • Get past papers from their official source.Past papers apne official source se hasil karein.

6) Health and sleep6) Sehat aur neend

7 to 8 hours of sleep and a little walking during the day help memory and focus. Avoid staying up all night before the exam.7 se 8 ghante ki neend aur din mein thori chehal qadmi yaadasht aur dhyan ke liye madadgar hai. Imtihan se pehle raat bhar jaagne se bachein.
