MDCAT ki Tayyari Kaise Karein: ek saaf study plan

Likhne wali: Javeria Maqsood, Rehnuma · Aakhri update: 2 November 2026

Ye guide ek aam study plan hai. MDCAT ka official syllabus, paper ka pattern aur tareekhein hamesha PMDC ki official website se dekhein, kyunke ye har saal badal sakti hain.

1) Pehle syllabus dekhein

Official syllabus download kar ke subject-wise topics ki list banayein (Biology, Chemistry, Physics, English aur Logical Reasoning). Har topic ke samne tick ka dabba rakhein.

2) Pehle concept, phir MCQ

Rate (ratta) lagane se pehle topic samjhein. Jab concept samajh aa jaye tab us topic ke MCQs karein. Is tareeqe se MCQ ke jawab yaad nahi karne parte, aap un ko nikal lete hain.

3) Roz ka chhota plan

  • 2 se 3 subjects mein baant kar parhein, roz ek hi subject nahi.
  • 25 minute parhai aur 5 minute aaram (Pomodoro) ke chhote blocks banayein.
  • Din ke aakhir mein 10 MCQs us topic ke karein jo aaj parha.

4) Kamzor topics ka register

Har ghalat MCQ ka topic ek copy mein likhein. Hafte mein ek din sirf in kamzor topics ko dobara karein. Rehnuma ke Progress section mein bhi aap ke kamzor topics nazar aate hain.

5) Revision aur mock tests

  • Har topic 1 din, 1 hafte aur 1 mahine baad dobara dekhein.
  • Imtihan se pehle waqt nigrani ke sath poore mock tests dein.
  • Past papers apne official source se hasil karein.

6) Sehat aur neend

7 se 8 ghante ki neend aur din mein thori chehal qadmi yaadasht aur dhyan ke liye madadgar hai. Imtihan se pehle raat bhar jaagne se bachein.
