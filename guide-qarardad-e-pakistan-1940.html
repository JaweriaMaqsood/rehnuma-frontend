What the resolution demandedQarardad mein kya mutalba tha

The demand was that the areas of India where Muslims are in a majority should be grouped so that independent states could be formed there, in which Muslims could live according to their religion, culture and political rights.Mutalba ye tha ke Hindustan ke wo ilaqe jahan musalman aksariyat mein hain, aise milaye jayein ke wahan azad riyasatein qaim ho saken, jin mein musalmano ko apne mazhab, saqafat aur siyasi haqooq ke mutabiq zindagi guzarne ka haq ho.

ImportanceAhmiyat

  • The resolution made the demand for a separate homeland for Muslims clear.Is qarardad se musalmano ke liye alag watan ka mutalba saaf ho gaya.
  • Opponents mockingly called it the "Pakistan Resolution", and that name became famous.Mukhalifeen ne isay mazaaq mein "Pakistan Resolution" kaha, aur ye naam hi mashhoor ho gaya.
  • Just 7 years later, on 14 August 1947, Pakistan came into being.Is ke baad sirf 7 saal mein, 14 August 1947 ko Pakistan wujood mein aaya.
  • In its memory the Minar-e-Pakistan was built in Lahore and Pakistan Day is celebrated on 23 March.Is ki yaad mein Lahore mein Minar-e-Pakistan bana aur 23 March ko Youm-e-Pakistan manaya jata hai.
