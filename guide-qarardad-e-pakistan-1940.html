SadaratQuaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah
Pesh karne waleA. K. Fazlul Huq (Bengal ke Chief Minister)
Taeed karne waleChaudhry Khaliquzzaman

Qarardad mein kya mutalba tha

Mutalba ye tha ke Hindustan ke wo ilaqe jahan musalman aksariyat mein hain, aise milaye jayein ke wahan azad riyasatein qaim ho saken, jin mein musalmano ko apne mazhab, saqafat aur siyasi haqooq ke mutabiq zindagi guzarne ka haq ho.

Ahmiyat

  • Is qarardad se musalmano ke liye alag watan ka mutalba saaf ho gaya.
  • Mukhalifeen ne isay mazaaq mein "Pakistan Resolution" kaha, aur ye naam hi mashhoor ho gaya.
  • Is ke baad sirf 7 saal mein, 14 August 1947 ko Pakistan wujood mein aaya.
  • Is ki yaad mein Lahore mein Minar-e-Pakistan bana aur 23 March ko Youm-e-Pakistan manaya jata hai.
