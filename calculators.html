Free Calculators for Pakistani Students

Free calculators useful for studying: scientific calculator, percentage, MDCAT and ECAT aggregate, and GPA/CGPA. No account or app needed, and they work on mobile too.Parhai me kaam aane wale free calculators: scientific calculator, percentage, MDCAT aur ECAT aggregate, aur GPA/CGPA. Koi account ya app ki zaroorat nahi, mobile par bhi chalte hain.

  • Scientific Calculator — A free online scientific calculator.Scientific Calculator — Ye free online scientific calculator hai.
  • Percentage Calculator — Find the percentage of your marks, the percent of a number, or a percent increase or decrease.Percentage Calculator — Is calculator se aap apne marks ka percentage, kisi number ka percent, ya percent me izafa/kami nikal sakti hain.
  • MDCAT Aggregate Calculator — A free MDCAT aggregate calculator.MDCAT Aggregate Calculator — Ye free MDCAT calculator (aggregate calculator for MDCAT) hai.
