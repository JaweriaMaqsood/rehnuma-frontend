Aksar poochhe jane wale sawal

Marks ka percentage kaise nikalte hain?

Formula: (obtained marks ÷ total marks) × 100. Jaise 850 marks 1100 me se: 850 ÷ 1100 × 100 = 77.27%.

Percent change kya hota hai?

Purani value se nayi value kitni badhi ya ghati. Formula: (nayi - purani) ÷ purani × 100.

Kya total marks sab boards me ek jaise hote hain?

Nahi. Punjab Matric me aam tor par 1200, FSc me 1100 hote hain, magar boards ke hisab se farq ho sakta hai. Apni result card dekhein.
