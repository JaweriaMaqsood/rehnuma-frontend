How do you find the percentage of marks?Marks ka percentage kaise nikalte hain?

Formula: (obtained marks ÷ total marks) × 100. For example 850 marks out of 1100: 850 ÷ 1100 × 100 = 77.27%.Formula: (obtained marks ÷ total marks) × 100. Jaise 850 marks 1100 me se: 850 ÷ 1100 × 100 = 77.27%.

What is percent change?Percent change kya hota hai?

How much the new value has increased or decreased compared with the old value. Formula: (new − old) ÷ old × 100.Purani value se nayi value kitni badhi ya ghati. Formula: (nayi - purani) ÷ purani × 100.

Are total marks the same in all boards?Kya total marks sab boards me ek jaise hote hain?

No. Punjab Matric is usually 1200 and FSc 1100, but it can differ from board to board. Check your result card.Nahi. Punjab Matric me aam tor par 1200, FSc me 1100 hote hain, magar boards ke hisab se farq ho sakta hai. Apni result card dekhein.
