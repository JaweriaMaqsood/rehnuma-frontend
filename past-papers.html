Past Papers aur Practice Papers

Board ke asli past papers un boards ki apni milkiyat hain, is liye hum unhein apni site par copy nahi karte. Neeche unhein hasil karne ka sahi tareeqa hai, aur Rehnuma ke apne free practice papers bhi hain.

Official past papers kahan se hasil karein

  • Matric aur Intermediate (Federal): FBISE ki website fbise.edu.pk par syllabus aur papers dekhein.
  • Matric aur Intermediate (Punjab): apne board ki website, jaise BISE Lahore biselahore.com.
  • Dusre boards: Karachi, Rawalpindi, Peshawar, Quetta wagaira ke liye Google par "(board ka naam) official website" likh kar sahi site kholein.
  • MDCAT: MDCAT ka official syllabus aur ittela PMDC ki website pmdc.pk par dekhein.
  • ECAT: engineering universities (jaise UET) ki official website par ECAT ka syllabus aur pattern dekhein.

Hamesha board ya university ki apni site se syllabus aur tareekhein check karein, kyunke ye har saal badal sakti hain.
