Where to get official past papersOfficial past papers kahan se hasil karein

  • Matric and Intermediate (Federal): see the syllabus and papers on the FBISE website fbise.edu.pk.Matric aur Intermediate (Federal): FBISE ki website fbise.edu.pk par syllabus aur papers dekhein.
  • Matric and Intermediate (Punjab): your own board's website, such as BISE Lahore biselahore.com.Matric aur Intermediate (Punjab): apne board ki website, jaise BISE Lahore biselahore.com.
  • Other boards: for Karachi, Rawalpindi, Peshawar, Quetta and so on, search Google for "(board name) official website" and open the correct site.Dusre boards: Karachi, Rawalpindi, Peshawar, Quetta wagaira ke liye Google par "(board ka naam) official website" likh kar sahi site kholein.
  • MDCAT: see the official MDCAT syllabus and notices on the PMDC website pmdc.pk.MDCAT: MDCAT ka official syllabus aur ittela PMDC ki website pmdc.pk par dekhein.
  • ECAT: see the ECAT syllabus and pattern on the official website of engineering universities (like UET).ECAT: engineering universities (jaise UET) ki official website par ECAT ka syllabus aur pattern dekhein.

Always check the syllabus and dates on the board's or university's own site, because they can change every year.Hamesha board ya university ki apni site se syllabus aur tareekhein check karein, kyunke ye har saal badal sakti hain.
