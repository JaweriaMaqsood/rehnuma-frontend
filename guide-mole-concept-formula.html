What is a mole?Mole kya hota hai

One mole is the amount of a substance that contains as many particles (atoms, molecules or ions) as there are atoms in 12 g of carbon-12. This number is Avogadro's number:Ek mole kisi cheez ke utne zarrat (atoms, molecules ya ions) ko kehte hain jitne 12 g carbon-12 mein atoms hote hain. Ye tadad Avogadro number hai:

NA = 6.022 × 10²³ per mole

Three useful formulasTeen kaam ke formulas

  • Moles = Mass (g) ÷ Molar mass (g/mol)
  • Number of particles = Moles × 6.022 × 10²³
  • Volume of a gas (at STP) = Moles × 22.4 dm³Gas ka volume (STP par) = Moles × 22.4 dm³

Some books also use 24 dm³ at room temperature, so check with your book or board.Kuch kitabon mein room temperature par 24 dm³ bhi use hota hai, is liye apni kitab ya board ke mutabiq check karein.

Molar mass of H₂O = 2(1) + 16 = 18 g/mol. Moles = 18 ÷ 18 = 1 mol. Molecules = 1 × 6.022 × 10²³ = 6.022 × 10²³.1) 18 g paani mein kitne molecules hain?
