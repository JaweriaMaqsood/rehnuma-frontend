1. She ___ to school every day.

  • A) go
  • B) goes
  • C) going
  • D) gone
Show answer

Correct answer: B) goes
With a third person singular subject (she), the simple present verb takes s/es.

1. She ___ to school every day.

  • A) go
  • B) goes
  • C) going
  • D) gone
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: B) goes
Third person singular (she) ke sath simple present me verb ke sath s/es lagta hai.

2. They ___ cricket now.

  • A) are playing
  • B) play
  • C) played
  • D) have played
Show answer

Correct answer: A) are playing
"Now" means it is happening at this moment, so the present continuous is used: are playing.

2. They ___ cricket now.

  • A) are playing
  • B) play
  • C) played
  • D) have played
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: A) are playing
"now" ka matlab abhi ho raha hai, isliye present continuous: are playing.

3. I ___ my homework yesterday.

  • A) do
  • B) have done
  • C) am doing
  • D) did
Show answer

Correct answer: D) did
"Yesterday" is past time, so the simple past "did" is used.

3. I ___ my homework yesterday.

  • A) do
  • B) have done
  • C) am doing
  • D) did
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: D) did
"yesterday" guzra hua waqt hai, to simple past ka "did" aata hai.

4. He has ___ his work.

  • A) finish
  • B) finishing
  • C) finished
  • D) finishes
Show answer

Correct answer: C) finished
In the present perfect, has/have is followed by the third form of the verb (finished).

4. He has ___ his work.

  • A) finish
  • B) finishing
  • C) finished
  • D) finishes
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: C) finished
Present perfect me has/have ke baad verb ki third form (finished) aati hai.

5. We ___ been waiting for an hour.

  • A) has
  • B) have
  • C) are
  • D) will
Show answer

Correct answer: B) have
Present perfect continuous: have/has + been + verb-ing. "We" takes have.

5. We ___ been waiting for an hour.

  • A) has
  • B) have
  • C) are
  • D) will
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: B) have
Present perfect continuous: have/has + been + verb-ing. We ke sath have aata hai.

6. By next year, she ___ graduated.

  • A) will have
  • B) has
  • C) had
  • D) is
Show answer

Correct answer: A) will have
Future perfect: will have + third form of the verb.

6. By next year, she ___ graduated.

  • A) will have
  • B) has
  • C) had
  • D) is
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: A) will have
Future perfect: will have + third form of verb.

7. The sun ___ in the east.

  • A) rise
  • B) rose
  • C) rising
  • D) rises
Show answer

Correct answer: D) rises
General truths use the simple present. "Sun" is third person singular, so: rises.

7. The sun ___ in the east.

  • A) rise
  • B) rose
  • C) rising
  • D) rises
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: D) rises
Aam sachai simple present me hoti hai. Sun third person singular hai, to rises.

8. I ___ TV when the light went out.

  • A) watch
  • B) am watching
  • C) was watching
  • D) have watched
Show answer

Correct answer: C) was watching
An action in progress in the past uses the past continuous: was/were + verb-ing.

8. I ___ TV when the light went out.

  • A) watch
  • B) am watching
  • C) was watching
  • D) have watched
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: C) was watching
Guzre hue waqt me jari kaam par past continuous: was/were + verb-ing.

9. "She has been studying since morning." Which tense is this?

  • A) Present continuous
  • B) Present perfect continuous
  • C) Past perfect
  • D) Simple present
Show answer

Correct answer: B) Present perfect continuous
has been + verb-ing is the present perfect continuous.

9. "She has been studying since morning." Ye kaun sa tense hai?

  • A) Present continuous
  • B) Present perfect continuous
  • C) Past perfect
  • D) Simple present
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: B) Present perfect continuous
has been + verb-ing present perfect continuous hai.

10. He ___ here since 2020.

  • A) has lived
  • B) lives
  • C) lived
  • D) is living
Show answer

Correct answer: A) has lived
"Since" is used with the present perfect: has/have + third form.

10. He ___ here since 2020.

  • A) has lived
  • B) lives
  • C) lived
  • D) is living
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: A) has lived
"since" ke sath present perfect aata hai: has/have + third form.

11. If it rains, we ___ at home.

  • A) stayed
  • B) would stay
  • C) stay
  • D) will stay
Show answer

Correct answer: D) will stay
First conditional: if + simple present, will + verb.

11. If it rains, we ___ at home.

  • A) stayed
  • B) would stay
  • C) stay
  • D) will stay
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: D) will stay
First conditional: if + simple present, will + verb.

12. "The train had left before we reached." Which tense is this?

  • A) Present perfect
  • B) Simple past
  • C) Past perfect
  • D) Past continuous
Show answer

Correct answer: C) Past perfect
had + third form is the past perfect. It shows the action that happened first of two past actions.

12. "The train had left before we reached." Ye kaun sa tense hai?

  • A) Present perfect
  • B) Simple past
  • C) Past perfect
  • D) Past continuous
Jawab dekhein

Sahi jawab: C) Past perfect
had + third form past perfect hai. Ye do guzre hue kamon me pehle hone wale kaam ko dikhata hai.
