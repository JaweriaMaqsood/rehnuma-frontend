Question 1. What is inertia?Sawal 1. Inertia kya hai?

Show sample answerNamoona jawab dekhein

The resistance of an object to any change in its state (rest or motion at constant speed) is called inertia. The greater the mass, the greater the inertia.Kisi jism ka apni halat (aaram ya ek jaisi raftaar ki harkat) mein tabdeeli ka muqabla karna inertia kehlata hai. Mass jitna zyada, inertia utna zyada.

Question 2. Write the difference between speed and velocity.Sawal 2. Speed aur velocity mein farq likhein.

Show sample answerNamoona jawab dekhein

Speed is only a magnitude (scalar), whereas velocity has a direction along with the magnitude, so it is a vector.Speed sirf miqdar (scalar) hai jabke velocity miqdar ke sath simt (direction) bhi rakhti hai, is liye ye vector hai.

Question 3. State Newton's second law and give the formula.Sawal 3. Newton ka doosra law likhein aur formula batayein.

Show sample answerNamoona jawab dekhein

The net force on an object equals the product of its mass and acceleration. Formula: F = ma.Jism par lagne wali net force us ke mass aur acceleration ke zarb ke barabar hoti hai. Formula: F = ma.
