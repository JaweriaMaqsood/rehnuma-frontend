Projectile Motion: time of flight, maximum height and range formulasProjectile Motion: time of flight, maximum height aur range ke formulas

Written by: Javeria Maqsood, Rehnuma · Last updated: 13 November 2026Likhne wali: Javeria Maqsood, Rehnuma · Aakhri update: 13 November 2026

When an object is thrown into the air and only gravity acts on it, its path is a parabola. Numericals on this are common in FSc Physics and ECAT.Jab koi cheez hawa mein phenki jaye aur sirf gravity ka asar ho, to us ka raasta parabola hota hai. FSc Physics aur ECAT mein is par numericals aksar aate hain.

Understand this firstPehle ye samajhein

The launch speed is v₀ and the angle is θ. The velocity has two components:Phenkne ki raftaar v₀ aur angle θ hai. Raftaar ke do hisse hote hain:

  • Horizontal: vₓ = v₀ cos θ (this stays constant throughout the flight)Horizontal: vₓ = v₀ cos θ (ye poore safar mein barabar rehta hai)
  • Vertical: vᵧ = v₀ sin θ (this changes because of gravity)Vertical: vᵧ = v₀ sin θ (gravity ki wajah se badalta hai)

At the highest point the vertical velocity is zero.Sab se unchi jagah par vertical raftaar zero hoti hai.

Assume: there is no air resistance and the object lands at the same height it was thrown from.Maan lein: hawa ka asar nahi hai aur cheez usi unchai par wapas girti hai jahan se phenki gayi thi.

Three key formulasTeen aham formulas

  • Time of flight: T = 2 v₀ sin θ ÷ g
  • Maximum height: H = v₀² sin²θ ÷ (2g)
  • Range: R = v₀² sin 2θ ÷ g

Here g = 9.8 m/s² (or 10 m/s² if the question gives it).Yahan g = 9.8 m/s² (ya sawal mein diye gaye 10 m/s²).

Solved numericalHal kiya hua numerical

Question: A ball is thrown at 20 m/s at an angle of 30°. Take g = 9.8 m/s². Find the time of flight, maximum height and range.Sawal: ek gend 20 m/s ki raftaar se 30° ke angle par phenki gayi. g = 9.8 m/s² lein. Time of flight, maximum height aur range nikalein.

  • T = 2 × 20 × sin 30° ÷ 9.8 = 2 × 20 × 0.5 ÷ 9.8 = 2.04 s
  • H = 20² × sin²30° ÷ (2 × 9.8) = 400 × 0.25 ÷ 19.6 = 5.10 m
  • R = 20² × sin 60° ÷ 9.8 = 400 × 0.866 ÷ 9.8 = 35.35 m

Why range is greatest at 45°45° par range sab se zyada kyun

The range formula is R = v₀² sin 2θ ÷ g. The largest value of sin 2θ is 1, which happens when 2θ = 90°, that is θ = 45°.Range ka formula R = v₀² sin 2θ ÷ g hai. sin 2θ ki sab se bari value 1 hai, jo tab hoti hai jab 2θ = 90°, yani θ = 45°.

Example: v₀ = 10 m/s, θ = 45°, g = 10 m/s² to R = 100 × 1 ÷ 10 = 10 m.

Two angles that add up to 90° (like 30° and 60°) give the same range.Do angle jo milkar 90° banate hon (jaise 30° aur 60°), ek jitna range dete hain.

Points to remember for the examImtihan ke liye yaad rakhne ki baatein

  • The horizontal velocity never changes.Horizontal raftaar kabhi nahi badalti.
  • At the highest point vᵧ = 0.Sab se unchi jagah par vᵧ = 0.
  • In a numerical, first split v₀ into vₓ and vᵧ, then apply the formula.Numerical mein pehle v₀ ko vₓ aur vᵧ mein todein, phir formula lagayein.
  • Always write the answer with its unit.Hamesha unit ke sath jawab likhein.
