Projectile Motion: time of flight, maximum height aur range ke formulas

Likhne wali: Javeria Maqsood, Rehnuma · Aakhri update: 13 November 2026

Jab koi cheez hawa mein phenki jaye aur sirf gravity ka asar ho, to us ka raasta parabola hota hai. FSc Physics aur ECAT mein is par numericals aksar aate hain.

Pehle ye samajhein

Phenkne ki raftaar v₀ aur angle θ hai. Raftaar ke do hisse hote hain:

  • Horizontal: vₓ = v₀ cos θ (ye poore safar mein barabar rehta hai)
  • Vertical: vᵧ = v₀ sin θ (gravity ki wajah se badalta hai)

Sab se unchi jagah par vertical raftaar zero hoti hai.

Maan lein: hawa ka asar nahi hai aur cheez usi unchai par wapas girti hai jahan se phenki gayi thi.

Teen aham formulas

  • Time of flight: T = 2 v₀ sin θ ÷ g
  • Maximum height: H = v₀² sin²θ ÷ (2g)
  • Range: R = v₀² sin 2θ ÷ g

Yahan g = 9.8 m/s² (ya sawal mein diye gaye 10 m/s²).

Hal kiya hua numerical

Sawal: ek gend 20 m/s ki raftaar se 30° ke angle par phenki gayi. g = 9.8 m/s² lein. Time of flight, maximum height aur range nikalein.

  • T = 2 × 20 × sin 30° ÷ 9.8 = 2 × 20 × 0.5 ÷ 9.8 = 2.04 s
  • H = 20² × sin²30° ÷ (2 × 9.8) = 400 × 0.25 ÷ 19.6 = 5.10 m
  • R = 20² × sin 60° ÷ 9.8 = 400 × 0.866 ÷ 9.8 = 35.35 m

45° par range sab se zyada kyun

Range ka formula R = v₀² sin 2θ ÷ g hai. sin 2θ ki sab se bari value 1 hai, jo tab hoti hai jab 2θ = 90°, yani θ = 45°.

Example: v₀ = 10 m/s, θ = 45°, g = 10 m/s² to R = 100 × 1 ÷ 10 = 10 m.

Do angle jo milkar 90° banate hon (jaise 30° aur 60°), ek jitna range dete hain.

Imtihan ke liye yaad rakhne ki baatein

  • Horizontal raftaar kabhi nahi badalti.
  • Sab se unchi jagah par vᵧ = 0.
  • Numerical mein pehle v₀ ko vₓ aur vᵧ mein todein, phir formula lagayein.
  • Hamesha unit ke sath jawab likhein.
