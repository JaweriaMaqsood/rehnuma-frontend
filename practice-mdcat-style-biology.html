Part 2: Short questions (3)Hissa 2: Mukhtasar sawal (3)

Question 1. Write one basic difference between mitosis and meiosis.Sawal 1. Mitosis aur meiosis mein ek bunyadi farq likhein.

Show sample answerNamoona jawab dekhein

Mitosis makes two identical diploid cells, whereas meiosis makes four haploid cells (gametes).Mitosis se do identical diploid cells banti hain, jabke meiosis se chaar haploid cells (gametes) banti hain.

Question 2. State the function of insulin.Sawal 2. Insulin ka kaam batayein.

Show sample answerNamoona jawab dekhein

Insulin lowers the amount of glucose in the blood and helps store it as glycogen in the liver and muscles.Insulin khoon mein glucose ki miqdar kam karta hai aur jigar aur muscles mein glycogen ke taur par jama karne mein madad deta hai.

Question 3. Write a feature of alveoli.Sawal 3. Alveoli ki khasusiyat likhein.

Show sample answerNamoona jawab dekhein

The walls of alveoli are very thin and have tiny blood vessels (capillaries) beside them, so gas exchange happens easily.Alveoli ki diwar bohot patli aur unki sath khoon ki chhoti naliyan (capillaries) hoti hain, jis se gas ka tabadla aasani se hota hai.
