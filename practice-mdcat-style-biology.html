Hissa 1: MCQs (10)

Q1. DNA mein guanine ke sath kaun sa base jorta hai?

  1. Adenine
  2. Thymine
  3. Cytosine
  4. Uracil
Jawab dekhein

C) Cytosine

Q2. Enzymes reaction ki kis cheez ko kam karte hain?

  1. Activation energy
  2. Temperature
  3. Products ki miqdar
  4. pH
Jawab dekhein

A) Activation energy

Q3. Universal donor blood group kaun sa hota hai?

  1. A positive
  2. AB positive
  3. O negative
  4. B negative
Jawab dekhein

C) O negative

Q4. Phephron mein gas ka tabadla kahan hota hai?

  1. Bronchi
  2. Alveoli
  3. Trachea
  4. Larynx
Jawab dekhein

B) Alveoli

Q5. Khoon mein glucose ki miqdar kam karne wala hormone kaun sa hai?

  1. Glucagon
  2. Insulin
  3. Adrenaline
  4. Thyroxine
Jawab dekhein

B) Insulin

Q6. Cell division ke kis marhale mein chromosomes equator par qatar mein aate hain?

  1. Prophase
  2. Metaphase
  3. Anaphase
  4. Telophase
Jawab dekhein

B) Metaphase

Q7. Gurde (kidney) ki functional unit kya hai?

  1. Neuron
  2. Nephron
  3. Alveolus
  4. Villus
Jawab dekhein

B) Nephron

Q8. Scurvy kis vitamin ki kami se hota hai?

  1. Vitamin A
  2. Vitamin B12
  3. Vitamin C
  4. Vitamin D
Jawab dekhein

C) Vitamin C

Q9. Dil ka kaun sa hissa oxygenated khoon poore jism mein bhejta hai?

  1. Right atrium
  2. Right ventricle
  3. Left atrium
  4. Left ventricle
Jawab dekhein

D) Left ventricle

Q10. Insan ke somatic cell mein chromosomes ki tadad kitni hoti hai?

  1. 23
  2. 46
  3. 44
  4. 48
Jawab dekhein

B) 46

Hissa 2: Mukhtasar sawal (3)

Sawal 1. Mitosis aur meiosis mein ek bunyadi farq likhein.

Namoona jawab dekhein

Mitosis se do identical diploid cells banti hain, jabke meiosis se chaar haploid cells (gametes) banti hain.

Sawal 2. Insulin ka kaam batayein.

Namoona jawab dekhein

Insulin khoon mein glucose ki miqdar kam karta hai aur jigar aur muscles mein glycogen ke taur par jama karne mein madad deta hai.

Sawal 3. Alveoli ki khasusiyat likhein.

Namoona jawab dekhein

Alveoli ki diwar bohot patli aur unki sath khoon ki chhoti naliyan (capillaries) hoti hain, jis se gas ka tabadla aasani se hota hai.
