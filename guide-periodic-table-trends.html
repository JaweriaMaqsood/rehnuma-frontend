Periodic Table Trends: atomic radius, ionization energy and electronegativityPeriodic Table Trends: atomic radius, ionization energy aur electronegativity

Written by: Javeria Maqsood, Rehnuma · Last updated: 13 November 2026Likhne wali: Javeria Maqsood, Rehnuma · Aakhri update: 13 November 2026

Periodic table trends appear in every Chemistry exam and in MDCAT. Once you understand the reason, there is no need to memorise.Periodic table ke trends Chemistry ke har imtihan aur MDCAT mein aate hain. Ek baar wajah samajh lein to rattay ki zaroorat nahi parti.

Understand two things firstPehle do baatein samjhein

  • Across a period (left to right): protons increase and electrons enter the same shell, so the pull of the nucleus increases.Period mein (left se right): proton barhte hain, electrons usi shell mein aate hain, is liye nucleus ki khinchai barhti hai.
  • Down a group (top to bottom): new shells are added, so the outer electrons move farther from the nucleus.Group mein (oopar se neeche): naye shells ate hain, is liye bahari electrons nucleus se door ho jate hain.

Atomic radius

The size of an atom.Atom ka size.

DirectionDishaAtomic radius
Across a period (left to right)Period mein left se rightDecreasesKam hota hai
Down a group (top to bottom)Group mein oopar se neecheIncreasesBarhta hai

Reason: across a period the nuclear charge increases and the nucleus pulls electrons in harder. Down a group the size increases as shells are added.Wajah: period mein nuclear charge barhta hai aur nucleus electrons ko zyada khinchta hai. Group mein shells barhne se size barhta hai.

Example: Na has a larger radius than Mg, and Br has a larger radius than Cl.Misal: Na ka radius Mg se bara hai, aur Br ka radius Cl se bara hai.

Ionization energy

The energy needed to remove one electron from an atom in the gas state.Gas halat mein atom se ek electron nikalne ke liye chahiye energy.

DirectionDishaIonization energy
Across a period (left to right)Period mein left se rightIncreasesBarhti hai
Down a group (top to bottom)Group mein oopar se neecheDecreasesKam hoti hai

Example: Li has a higher ionization energy than Cs, because Li's outer electron is closer to the nucleus.Misal: Li ki ionization energy Cs se zyada hai, kyunke Li ka bahari electron nucleus ke qareeb hai.

Some exceptions: Be has a higher ionization energy than B, and N has a higher one than O. These are often asked in exams.Kuch exceptions: Be ki ionization energy B se zyada hai, aur N ki O se zyada hai. Imtihan mein ye aksar poochte hain.

Electronegativity

The ability to pull shared electrons in a bond towards itself.Bond mein shared electrons ko apni taraf khinchne ki salahiyat.

DirectionDishaElectronegativity
Across a period (left to right)Period mein left se rightIncreasesBarhti hai
Down a group (top to bottom)Group mein oopar se neecheDecreasesKam hoti hai

The most electronegative element is fluorine (F).Sab se zyada electronegative element fluorine (F) hai.

Metallic character and ionsMetallic character aur ions

  • Metallic character decreases towards the right of a period and increases down a group (like atomic radius).Metallic character period mein right ki taraf kam hota hai aur group mein neeche ki taraf barhta hai (atomic radius ki tarah).
  • A cation (positive ion) is smaller than its atom.Cation (positive ion) apne atom se chota hota hai.
  • An anion (negative ion) is larger than its atom.Anion (negative ion) apne atom se bara hota hai.

An easy way to rememberYaad rakhne ka asaan tareeqa

  • Radius: decreases to the right, increases going down.Radius: right par kam, neeche barhta.
  • Ionization energy and electronegativity: increase to the right, decrease going down.Ionization energy aur electronegativity: right par barhti, neeche kam hoti.
  • First see the direction, then write the reason: nuclear charge or shells.Pehle disha dekhein, phir wajah likhein: nuclear charge ya shells.

Frequently asked questionsAksar poochhe jane wale sawal

Why does atomic radius decrease across a period?Atomic radius period mein kyun kam hota hai?

Because as protons increase the nuclear charge increases and electrons are pulled closer to the nucleus in the same shell.Kyunke proton barhne se nuclear charge barhta hai aur electrons usi shell mein nucleus ke zyada qareeb khinch jate hain.

Which element is the most electronegative?Sab se zyada electronegative element konsa hai?

Fluorine.

Why does ionization energy decrease down a group?Ionization energy group mein neeche kyun kam hoti hai?

The outer electron is farther from the nucleus, so it is easier to remove.Bahari electron nucleus se door hota hai, is liye usay nikalna aasan hota hai.
