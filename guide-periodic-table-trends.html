Periodic Table Trends: atomic radius, ionization energy aur electronegativity

Likhne wali: Javeria Maqsood, Rehnuma · Aakhri update: 13 November 2026

Periodic table ke trends Chemistry ke har imtihan aur MDCAT mein aate hain. Ek baar wajah samajh lein to rattay ki zaroorat nahi parti.

Pehle do baatein samjhein

  • Period mein (left se right): proton barhte hain, electrons usi shell mein aate hain, is liye nucleus ki khinchai barhti hai.
  • Group mein (oopar se neeche): naye shells ate hain, is liye bahari electrons nucleus se door ho jate hain.

Atomic radius

Atom ka size.

DishaAtomic radius
Period mein left se rightKam hota hai
Group mein oopar se neecheBarhta hai

Wajah: period mein nuclear charge barhta hai aur nucleus electrons ko zyada khinchta hai. Group mein shells barhne se size barhta hai.

Misal: Na ka radius Mg se bara hai, aur Br ka radius Cl se bara hai.

Ionization energy

Gas halat mein atom se ek electron nikalne ke liye chahiye energy.

DishaIonization energy
Period mein left se rightBarhti hai
Group mein oopar se neecheKam hoti hai

Misal: Li ki ionization energy Cs se zyada hai, kyunke Li ka bahari electron nucleus ke qareeb hai.

Kuch exceptions: Be ki ionization energy B se zyada hai, aur N ki O se zyada hai. Imtihan mein ye aksar poochte hain.

Electronegativity

Bond mein shared electrons ko apni taraf khinchne ki salahiyat.

DishaElectronegativity
Period mein left se rightBarhti hai
Group mein oopar se neecheKam hoti hai

Sab se zyada electronegative element fluorine (F) hai.

Metallic character aur ions

  • Metallic character period mein right ki taraf kam hota hai aur group mein neeche ki taraf barhta hai (atomic radius ki tarah).
  • Cation (positive ion) apne atom se chota hota hai.
  • Anion (negative ion) apne atom se bara hota hai.

Yaad rakhne ka asaan tareeqa

  • Radius: right par kam, neeche barhta.
  • Ionization energy aur electronegativity: right par barhti, neeche kam hoti.
  • Pehle disha dekhein, phir wajah likhein: nuclear charge ya shells.
