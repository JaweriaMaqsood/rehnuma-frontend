How do I switch between degrees and radians?Degree aur radian me kaise badlein?

Click the "DEG" button at the top of the calculator and it changes to "RAD". Trigonometry answers (sin, cos, tan) follow the selected mode.Calculator ke upar wale button "DEG" par click karein, wo "RAD" ho jata hai. Trigonometry (sin, cos, tan) ke jawab usi mode ke mutabiq aate hain.

Can I use it in a board exam?Kya ye board imtihan me use ho sakta hai?

Does it give correct sin, cos, tan answers?Kya sin, cos, tan ka sahi jawab milta hai?
